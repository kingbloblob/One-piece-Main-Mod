# Rocks D. Xebec: research notes and how he works in the mod

## Who he is in One Piece

- **The most feared pirate before Roger.** He was captain of the Rocks Pirates, a crew of future legends: Whitebeard, Big Mom, Kaido, Shiki, Captain John, Wang Zhi and others. They fought each other as often as their enemies.
- **His goal:** he said he wanted to become "King of the World" and "take the head of God". Recent chapters (around 1159 to 1162) recast this. At God Valley he was fighting Imu, and he went there to rescue his wife Eris and his son Teach.
- **Family:** he belongs to the Davy clan, and it is widely read that his son Teach is Blackbeard. Reports of chapter 1154 say he was the original user of the **Yami Yami no Mi**, the darkness fruit Blackbeard has now. Some of this still comes from spoiler summaries, so treat it as likely rather than final.
- **Feats:** reports say he killed a Marine Admiral and defeated Figarland Garling's Holy Knights. At God Valley he also fought his own father and grandmother, whom Imu had turned into monsters.
- **God Valley, 38 years ago:** a beautiful island where the Celestial Dragons held a "native hunting" tournament. The Rocks Pirates attacked it, and Roger and Garp teamed up to stop them. Afterward the island was erased from the map and the Rocks Pirates broke up. Exactly how Xebec fell (Imu, or Roger and Garp, or both) is still not fully shown.
- **Bounty:** never revealed in canon.

## How he works in the mod

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Optional superboss, the hardest fight in the mod. The story never requires him. |
| Arena | **God Valley**, a ruined holy island (structure `god_valley`, being built by the worldgen part of the mod). Sea Charts and the Locate tools can find it. |
| Stats | Tier 5, **1200 health** (Imu 800, Kaido 400), **28 damage**, 1.8x size, netherite sword |
| Attacks | Every special attack is a random pick from darkness pull, Whitebeard-style quake, Conqueror's Haki, a knockback punch and a sword sweep. On Hard and Expert he also uses Conqueror's Haki as his second attack, and on Expert he gets a rage at 30% health. |
| Drops | Yami Yami no Mi, 64 Berries (x2 Hard, x4 Expert), lots of XP, his own Wanted Poster (so you can rematch him on Hard, then Expert), and netherite, Nether Stars and enchanted golden apples on Hard and Expert |
| Bounty | 6,000,000,000 Berries in the bounty system (a game value, since canon never gave one) |
| Poster | Sengoku doesn't sell it. You get it the first time you beat him at God Valley. |
