One Piece mod · story campaign design

The Road to Laugh Tale

A main story that walks you through the voyage in order: East Blue, the first half of the Grand Line, the Summit War and the two-year training, the New World, and a finale on Laugh Tale. It uses the characters, lairs, crew, Haki, styles, ships and navigation the mod already has, and adds a Captain's Log that always tells you what to do next.

Hours by saga

"Fast" is a player who follows the Log, skips optional fights and only hires the crew roles the story needs. "Thorough" does every quest giver in that saga, hires the full crew, learns two styles and explores the lairs. Travel is counted in: legendary islands sit about 1,000 to 4,000 blocks apart.

How the campaign works

Everything lives in a new package, com.onepiecemod.story. Other parts of the mod only get a one-line hook where the story needs to know something happened or needs to hold something back.

Captain's Log

You get it on first join, right after picking a race. Right-click it, or type /story, to see the current chapter, a short story text, and a checklist of goals. Each chapter hands you a Sea Chart to the next place, so you are never lost.

Goals reuse what exists

  • Beat a boss (boss list)
  • Finish a quest giver's step, or meet someone
  • Reach a structure, or a sea (Calm Belt, Grand Line)
  • Bounty, Haki level, fruit mastery, Log Pose islands
  • Hire a crew role, learn a style, craft or carry an item
  • Survive a story event (new)

Story events (new)

A few set pieces that plain quests can't do: escape Kizaru on Sabaody, hold the line at Marineford against Marine waves, and the Road Poneglyph rubbings. They reuse the Marine squads and boss entities.

Story gates

Some shortcuts get held back until the right chapter (see the pacing problems below). A world option, storyGates, turns them off for sandbox players and servers that want the old free play.

Pacing problems in the current mod

Found by reading the data files and code. Each comes with the fix I'd make; fixes marked "gate" only apply while story gates are on.

Too fast

Wanted Posters skip the whole voyage

Sengoku sells every boss poster (6 to 64 Berries), and quest "defeat" steps hand you the poster. You can summon Kaido in your backyard, so lairs, ships and navigation become optional.

Fix (gate): the first win against each story boss has to be at its lair. Posters become rematches, which fits the Hard and Expert rematch versions being added. Sengoku only sells posters for bosses you already beat, and quest steps give the Sea Chart to the lair instead of the poster.

Too fast

Awakening only needs 5 cheap boss kills

"Beat 5 different bosses" can be 5 posters of Buggy-tier bosses bought for about 100 Berries, so awakening lands in the first few hours.

Fix (gate): awakening needs the Wano chapter done, on top of the existing 1,000 mastery.

Out of order

Early characters send you to endgame places

Luffy's second step is "talk to Zoro", but Zoro lives at Kuraigana Castle, Mihawk's legendary lair. Jinbe asks for Big Mom at step 3, Bonney for Kizaru, Garp for Aokiji. A new player following the first quest giver walks into the hardest areas.

Fix: the Log sends you to quest givers only for steps that fit your saga, and shows later steps as "come back after Wano". In characters.json, move Zoro's home to an early place (he starts at Shells Town in the story) or change Luffy's step to "hire a swordsman".

No ending

Nothing ties the systems together, and Laugh Tale is just another structure

There is no main goal or finish line. Laugh Tale generates naturally and /locate can find it, so the final island could be the first one you see.

Fix (gate): the campaign itself, and Laugh Tale's treasure and Roger only open once you carry the four Road Poneglyph rubbings.

Map

The world has no East Blue to start in

The Grand Line is the stripe from Z −1000 to 1000, so world spawn is already in the Grand Line. Structures of every tier spawn anywhere their biome fits, so Wano can sit next to Foosha Village.

Fix: the story doesn't depend on geography. Each chapter's Sea Chart points to the nearest copy of the right place. Optionally, the sea stripes can move so spawn sits in East Blue (one number in CalmBelt.java, owned by the seas work). Without that, chapter 9 asks you to cross a Calm Belt and come back.

Too fast

Rayleigh's Haki lessons are cheap

10 Berries for 40 XP on both Haki at once. About 750 Berries (one tenth of all quest rewards) buys Armament and Observation level 5.

Fix (gate): lessons open at Sabaody, cost rises each time (10, 15, 20...) and stop above level 3. Levels 4 and 5 come from real fighting.

Farmable

Conqueror's Haki can be farmed with rematches

Every boss kill has a 2 to 10% chance, and posters make kills repeatable.

Fix (gate): only a first win against each boss rolls the chance, plus the guaranteed trial at Amazon Lily.

Luck spike

Any leaf can drop Gura Gura on day one

The 1-in-2,000 leaf drop picks from all 107 fruits evenly, so an Emperor's fruit can show up in the first hour.

Fix (gate): leaves only drop fruits whose tier matches your saga or lower. Bosses still drop their own.

Blocked

No way up to the sky islands

Angel Beach, Weatheria and Shandora sit at y=170. Without Elytra or a fruit that flies, survival players can't reach Enel.

Fix: a one-time Knock Up Stream launch from Cricket's house in chapter 12, plus a cloud ladder home.

Fine

Things that already pace well

Crew hire prices (940 Berries for all nine), the crafting station chain, the fighting style final moves locked behind bosses, Marine hunt threat levels, and the 1,000 mastery for awakening. The story uses these as they are.

What I'd build, in order

  1. Core: Captain's Log, /story, the 26 chapters as a data file you can edit, goal checks, rewards and Sea Charts.
  2. Story gates on the shortcuts above, with the storyGates world option to turn them off.
  3. Story events: Kizaru escape, Marineford siege, Knock Up Stream, Road Poneglyph rubbings, the Laugh Tale ending.
  4. Data fixes in other parts: Zoro's home, Luffy's step 2, and the optional sea stripe move (I'd ask those owners first).
