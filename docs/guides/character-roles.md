# One Piece characters: who does what

Every character also has a **Wanted Poster** that spawns a boss version of them (for fun fights in creative mode).
Beating any boss drops Berries and that character's Devil Fruit (once the fruit item exists).

**Finding people and places:** a Vivre Card slides toward its owner's home (right-click it), and the owner appears when you get there. A Sea Chart turns into a treasure map with an X on a One Piece location. Quest steps hand these out: "go talk to" steps give the Vivre Card, boss steps give a Sea Chart to the boss's lair.

Stats by tier: 1 = 40 HP / 4 dmg, 2 = 80 / 6, 3 = 150 / 9, 4 = 250 / 13, 5 = 400 / 18.

## Bosses (25)

**Rematches:** beat a boss and their Wanted Poster brings them back on **Hard** (2x health, 1.5x damage, armor, a second attack, faster attacks), then **Expert** (3.5x health, 2x damage, more armor, Conqueror's Haki every third attack, and a rage at 30% health). Hard pays 2x Berries, Expert 4x, plus XP orbs and extra prizes (up to a Nether Star on tier 5 Expert). Sneak while using the poster for a Normal fight.

## Quest givers (22)

Talk to a quest giver to start their quest line. Finish a step, talk to them again, and the next step starts right away.

### Koby

_Luffy's first friend. Gives the very first quest._ Lives at Shells Town Marine Base.

1. Go talk to Monkey D. Luffy (reward: 10 Berries)
2. Defeat Buggy (reward: 20 Berries + 1 iron sword)
3. Go talk to Monkey D. Garp (reward: 20 Berries)

### X Drake

_Marine spy hidden in Kaido's crew. Wants Hawkins stopped._ Last seen at Navarone (G-8) Fortress.

1. Go talk to Koby (reward: 15 Berries)
2. Defeat Basil Hawkins (reward: 80 Berries + 16 experience bottle)
3. Go talk to Monkey D. Garp (reward: 50 Berries)

### Jewelry Bonney

3. Defeat Borsalino (Kizaru) (reward: 300 Berries)

3. Defeat Basil Hawkins (reward: 80 Berries)

## Traders (9)

Traders level up like villagers: every trade gives them XP, and at Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert and Master they unlock new trades. Each trade runs out after a few uses and restocks every Minecraft day.
