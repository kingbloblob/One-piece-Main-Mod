# Farming and ranching

Everything is in the creative tab **One Piece: Farming & Ranching**.

## Island crops

14 crops, each from its own island. A crop grows at a normal pace on its home island and only about a
third as fast anywhere else, so the full set means sailing the Grand Line (or farming on each island).

| Crop | Home island | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| East Blue Cabbage | Conomi, Orange Town, Loguetown | Baratie Cabbage Rolls |
| Wano Rice | Wano, Kuri, Onigashima | Omurice, Paella, Kuja Curry, Truffle Risotto, onigiri, sake |
| Kibi Millet | Wano, Kuri | dango (crafting table) |
| Cloud Wheat | Skypiea | Cloud Bread |
| Elbaph Barley | Elbaph | Giant's Ale, bread |
| Frost Turnip | Drum Island, frozen Punk Hazard | Frost Turnip Stew |
| Jungle Yam | Jaya, Little Garden | Jungle Yam Stew |
| Dressrosa Tomato | Dressrosa, Green Bit | Omurice, Dressrosa Paella |
| Snake Pepper | Amazon Lily | Kuja Curry |
| Sea Grapes | Fishman Island, Sabaody | Sea Grape Salad |
| Totland Cacao | Whole Cake, Cacao Island | Totland Chocolate |
| Mink Tea | Zou | Mink Tea |
| Ghost Pumpkin | Thriller Bark, Kuraigana, Withered Forest | Ghost Pumpkin Pie |
| Sand Melon | Alabasta | Sand Melon Sorbet |

**Finding seeds:** break grass, ferns and wild plants on a crop's home island; about 1 in 12 drops a seed.
A grown crop drops 1 to 3 of the crop plus seeds. Any crop can also be crafted back into 2 seeds.
Villagers will plant them too.

## Seed Vault

Right-click it with an island seed to bank that kind (it keeps one seed). From then on, right-click with an
empty hand to see your catalog and click **[Take a seed]** for one free seed of each banked kind per day
(also `/seedvault`). Rewards: 3 kinds = 16 bone meal, 6 = 2 Dial Sprinklers, 9 = 16 Ranch Manure,
all 14 = 4 Dial Sprinklers and an enchanted golden apple.

## Irrigation

- **Sprinkler** (copper): wets farmland 3 blocks around it (7x7) every 5 seconds and gives crops extra
  growth, about twice as fast overall. Needs water touching it or 2 blocks under it.
- **Dial Sprinkler** (Skypiea dial shells): 5 blocks around (11x11), no water needed.
- **Watering Can**: right-click water to fill (39 uses). Right-click farmland or a crop: waters a 3x3 patch
  and each crop has a 1 in 3 chance to grow a step.
- **Ranch Manure**: use on a crop and every crop in a 5x5 patch grows a step.

## Ranching

Place a **Ranch Trough** and fill it with fodder (wheat, hay bales = 9, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, apples,
melon, kelp, sugar cane, berries, grass and any island crop). Sneak-click pours the whole stack.
Grown animals within 8 blocks eat from it. About every 3 minutes each one leaves something, and
sometimes manure too. Put a chest under or beside the trough and it all goes in there.

| Animal | Gives |
|---|---|
| Cow, goat, mooshroom, Drum Reindeer | Fresh Milk |
| Chicken | Egg |
| Sheep | Wool of its colour |
| Pig | Truffle |
| Rabbit | Rabbit hide |
| South Bird | South Bird Egg |
| Spot-billed Duck | Giant Duck Egg |
| Cotton Candy Lamb | Cotton Candy |
| Fuzza | Feathers |
| Hiking Bear | Honeycomb |
| Sauropod, Long Horse, anything else | Ranch Manure |

Farm animals by a fed trough also breed on their own, up to 10 of a kind.
Fresh Milk can also be made from a milk bucket and 3 glass bottles.

## Crew farm plots

Place a **Straw Hat Scarecrow** and set any crewmate to Work within 6 blocks of it. Whatever their role,
they farm there first: harvest and replant grown crops (helping two neighbours grow too), sow empty farmland
with seeds from their bag or the nearest chest, top up Ranch Troughs with spare crops, and put the harvest in
the chest. When there is nothing to do they go back to their usual job.

## Farm dishes (Ship's Galley)

15 dishes with long buffs, like Sanji's fish dishes (half as long again with a Cook in your crew):
Omurice, Cloud Bread, Sand Melon Sorbet, Frost Turnip Stew (Winter Islands can't freeze you),
Totland Chocolate, Ghost Pumpkin Pie, Dressrosa Paella, Giant's Ale, Mink Tea, Sea Grape Salad, Kuja Curry,
Jungle Yam Stew, Baratie Cabbage Rolls, Truffle Risotto (needs a Cook) and Sanji's Ranch Breakfast (needs a
Cook). Cheese is made from 2 Fresh Milk at the Galley.
