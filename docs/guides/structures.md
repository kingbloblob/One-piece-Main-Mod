# One Piece structures and locations

All 80 structures in the mod, grouped by difficulty. Every one has a loot chest. Bosses drop Berries and their Devil Fruit.
Find any of them with `/locate structure onepiece:<id>`.

How often they appear: easy places are common, legendary ones are rare (about one per 64 x 64 chunks, wherever the biome fits).
They spawn in normal worlds and in the Grand Line world type.

## Guards

Guards are normal Minecraft mobs dressed up as One Piece fighters. Their gear and health are in
`src/main/resources/onepiece/guards.json`; edit it to make them easier or harder.

| Difficulty | Guards |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Mountain Bandit, Rookie Pirate, Buggy Pirate, Marine Recruit, Kuja Warrior (zombies and skeletons) |
| Medium | Marine Soldier, Marine Rifleman, Pirate, Pirate Gunner, Fish-Man, Zombie Soldier, Baroque Works Agent, Bounty Hunter, Homie, Samurai, Frost Soldier |
| Hard | Marine Captain, CP Agent, Gifter, Headliner, Impel Down Jailer, Humandrill, Gladiator, Donquixote Pirate, Whitebeard/Blackbeard Pirate, Flame Guard, Sky Priest, War Beast |
| Legendary | Vice Admiral, Pacifista, Seraphim, CP0 Agent, Jailer Beast, Ancient Guardian |
