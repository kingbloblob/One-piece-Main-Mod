# Taming, riding and pets

## How to tame
| Creature | How | Ride it? |
|---|---|---|
| Sea King | Beat it below 25% health, then right-click it with **Sea King Bait** (1 in 3 bites works) | Yes, through the sea. Can pull your ship |
| Punk Hazard Dragon | Beat it below 30% health, then feed it **magma cream** | **Yes, it flies** |
| Fuzza (Skypiea's giant bird) | Feed it **wheat seeds** | **Yes, it flies** |
| Bananawani | Beat it below 30% health, then feed it **raw beef** | Yes (swims at the surface) |
| Lapahn | Beat it below 30% health, then feed it **carrots** | Yes, jumps high |
| Kung-Fu Dugong | Feed it **cod** | Yes |
| Karoo (Spot-Billed Duck) | Feed it **wheat seeds** | Yes, very fast |
| Fighting Bull | Feed it **wheat** | Yes, and it rams |
| Yagara Bull, Megalo | Feed them **kelp / cod** | Yes, through the sea |
| Island Whale (Laboon's kind) | Feed it **cod** | Yes, through the sea |
| Momoo (sea cow) | Feed it **cod** | Yes, through the sea. Can pull your ship |
| Cerberus (Thriller Bark) | Beat it below 30% health, then feed it **rotten flesh** | No, it guards you |

| South Bird, Cotton Candy Lamb, Den Den Mushi, Hiking Bear, Circus Lion, Battle Snake, Kraken | Their favourite food | No, they follow you |

Each feeding has a 1 in 3 chance. Hearts mean it's yours.

## Riding
- Right-click your pet with an empty hand to climb on. Steer with WASD and the mouse. Sneak to get off.
- Land mounts: hold **Space** to charge a jump (jump bar above the hotbar), let go to jump.
- Sea mounts (Sea King, Yagara Bull, Megalo...): they swim along the surface so you stay dry. Look down while moving to dive, look up to come back up. You never run out of air on their back. (Devil Fruit users still get weak once they're under the sea.)

## Flying mounts (new)
Fuzza and the Punk Hazard Dragon fly.
- **Hold Space** to take off and climb (works from the ground or from the water).
- **W** flies where you look: look up to climb, look down to dive.
- Let go of everything and it glides down slowly.
- It lands softly, and you take no fall damage riding it. If you jump off in mid-air you float down (Slow Falling).
- Fuzza is quick to tame (wheat seeds) but small and weak. The dragon is a fight first (150 health, fire bite), then the strongest mount in the game.

## Sea Kings pull your ship (new)
Like the two Sea Kings that pull Boa Hancock's ship across the Calm Belt.
1. Craft a **Towing Harness**: 3 leather on top, iron ingot + iron chain + iron ingot in the middle, 2 leather at the bottom corners.
2. Stand on (or next to) your ship and right-click your tamed **Sea King** or **Momoo** with it.
3. It swims ahead of the bow and pulls. Take the helm and sail as normal.
- One hauler: +15% speed in normal wind. In the **Calm Belt** (no wind at all) your ship would only crawl, but a Sea King pulls it at about 2.2x, two Sea Kings at about 3.4x.
- Up to two can pull one ship; they swim side by side.
- Right-click it with the harness again to unhitch it.

## Bond levels (new)
Your pets grow closer to you the more you do together. Levels go from 1 to 10.
- **Fighting**: +3 bond for every kill it makes (+10 for a big one with 100+ health).
- **Riding** or **pulling your ship**: +1 every 30 seconds.
- **Feeding** it its favourite food while it's hurt: +1 (once a minute).

Each level past 1 gives the pet **+6% health and +5% attack**, and ridden pets run a little faster. At **bond 5** its ability gets stronger ("II": higher effect level, shorter cooldowns, harder hits). At **bond 10** it's a Legendary partner (+54% health, +45% attack). Level 2 needs 20 bond, level 5 needs 200, level 10 needs 900.

The Pet Whistle shows each pet's bond level; **/pets** shows the exact numbers.

## Pets
- Pets follow you, fight for you (if they can fight) and never despawn, not even hostile ones on Peaceful.
- Sneak + right-click a pet with an empty hand: stay here / follow me.
- **Pet Whistle** (copper ingot + iron nugget + string): call every pet within 128 blocks and see their health and bond. Sneak + use: they all wait. Sea pets only come if there's water near you.
- Pets heal slowly when nothing has hurt them for 10 seconds. You get a chat message (with coordinates) if one dies.
- You can't hurt your own pet by accident. Sneak while attacking if you mean it.
- Sea King Bait is craftable: cod + salmon + 2 rotten flesh = 2 bait. Use it over deep water to call a Sea King.
- **Pet Bed** and **Pet Bowl** (furniture): a tamed pet within 4 blocks of either heals three times as fast.

## New achievements
- **Wings of Skypiea**: fly high on a tamed Fuzza or Punk Hazard Dragon.
- **Kuja Style**: harness a Sea King (or Momoo) to your ship.
- **Partners for Life**: reach bond level 10 with a pet.

## Commands
- `/pets`: your pets nearby, with health, bond and ability.
- `/pets abilities`: every creature's ability.
- `/pets maxbond` (operators, for testing): max out the bond of your pets within 32 blocks.
