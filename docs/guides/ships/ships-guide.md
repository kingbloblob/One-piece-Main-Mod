# Famous ships: the Going Merry, the Thousand Sunny and the ships you meet at sea

The ships of the show are in the mod as full-size block builds that sail as one piece. You can walk their decks when
docked, open their chests, fire their cannons and sail them like any ship you build yourself.

## Your ships

| Ship | How you get it | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Going Merry** | Finish the story chapter **Syrup Village** (Romance Dawn saga). Kaya's gift, like in the show. | The Straw Hats' little caravel: sheep's head on the bow, white bulwarks, the Straw Hat sail, Sanji's galley in the stern, the three mikan trees on the poop deck, the red-and-white striped stern sail. About 34 blocks long. |
| **Thousand Sunny** | Finish the story chapter **Water 7 and Enies Lobby** (beat Rob Lucci). Franky's work, built from Adam Wood. | The big brig sloop: lion's head with the sun mane, lawn deck with the tree and swing, kitchen and dining room, aquarium bar, library, the round observation room on the mainmast, Soldier Dock hatches on both sides. About 47 blocks long. |

Both arrive as an **item**. Stand by the sea, look at deep open water and right-click: the ship appears ahead of you,
full size, pointing the way you look. It needs open sea about half its length all around (at least 4 blocks deep), so
row out past the shallows first. Then right-click the ship to climb aboard; the first one aboard takes the wheel.

Everything that works on a built ship works on these: W/S/A/D to sail, right-click chests and the kitchen while sailing,
C to fire the cannons, right-click twice to drop anchor (the ship turns back into blocks you can walk around in and
change), and right-click the Ship's Wheel to set sail again. If you rebuild part of her while docked, she keeps the change.

The Sunny's lawn and the Merry's planters are made of **Deck Lawn** and **Deck Soil**: they look and work like grass
and dirt (plants grow on them), but they count as part of the ship. Plain grass and dirt never sail, because the game
treats them as land. Use Deck Lawn and Deck Soil for gardens on your own ships too.

Lose her? Operators can give another with `/give @s onepiece:going_merry` or `/give @s onepiece:thousand_sunny`.

## Ships you meet at sea

Sea encounters (every few minutes while sailing) and raids now bring the real ships:

| Ship | Who | Where (enemy tier) |
|---|---|---|
| **Marine Warship** | Marines | Patrols everywhere (tiers 0-2): white hull, blue band, seagull bow, MARINE sails, two masts |
| **Marine Battleship** | Marines | Wanted pirates in rough seas (tiers 3-5): three masts, two gun decks, three-storey stern castle |
| **Big Top** | Buggy Pirates | East Blue (tiers 0-1): the circus tent on deck, elephant figurehead |
| **Miss Love Duck** | Alvida Pirates | East Blue (tier 0): pink, duck figurehead |
| **Bezan Black** | Black Cat Pirates | East Blue (tier 1): black ship, cat's head |
| **Polar Tang** | Heart Pirates | Grand Line (tiers 2-3): Law's yellow submarine, surfaced |
| **Victoria Punk** | Kid Pirates | New World (tiers 3-5): skull bow, iron plates, red sails |
| **Red Force** | Red Hair Pirates | New World (tiers 4-5): Shanks' red flagship, dragon figurehead |

Board one (right-click alongside, or a Boarding Hook), beat the crew, and she is yours to keep, cannons, chests and all.

## For operators and testers

- `/ship list` names every ship the game knows, with its size.
- `/ship <id>` puts that ship on the sea 40 blocks ahead of you (ids: going_merry, thousand_sunny, marine_warship,
  marine_battleship, big_top, red_force, polar_tang, victoria_punk, miss_love_duck, bezan_black).
- `/seabattle pirate 0` and `/seabattle marine 3` still call enemy ships by tier.

## Adding or changing a ship (for builders)

Ships are data: `data/onepiece/structure/ships/<id>.nbt` (the blocks) plus `data/onepiece/ships/<id>.json` (name, which
way the bow points, how deep it sits, who sails it and at which tiers). A data pack can add more the same way. The
mod's own ships are generated by `tools/ships/build.py <id>` from `tools/ships/<id>.py`, using `tools/ships/shipkit.py`;
`tools/ships/SPECS.md` describes what each ship looks like in the show. Put one Ship's Wheel on the deck facing the bow,
keep the sea surface at y 0 and the bow toward +x, never use plain grass or dirt (use onepiece:deck_lawn and
onepiece:deck_soil), and run the build script to get previews and the template.
