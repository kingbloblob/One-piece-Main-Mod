# Furniture & Props guide

108 furniture and prop blocks for detailed builds (Creative tab "One Piece: Furniture", Crafter's Journal tab "Furniture & Props"). Every one is made at a crafting table.

- Chairs, stools, benches, armchairs, thrones and floor cushions: right-click with an empty hand to sit, sneak to stand up.
- Wall shelves, life rings, nets, tool and weapon racks, notice boards hang on a wall (place against the wall). The hanging rope hangs from a ceiling.
- Rope ladders and hanging ropes can be climbed. Curtains and nets can be walked through.
- Everything turns to face you when placed and can be placed under water.
