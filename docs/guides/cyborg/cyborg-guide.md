# Pacifista and Seraphim hunts, and cyborg upgrades

Vegapunk's war machines roam the second half of the Grand Line. Beat them for parts, then rebuild your body like Franky.

## The hunts

Every minute, a player in the right waters may be found by a hunt (8%, or 18% at Sabaody and Egghead). After one shows up, there's a 10 minute break before the next. Hunts don't start in Peaceful, in Creative, near your bed, or while a raid, boss fight or story event is on.

| Unit | Where | When | Health | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pacifista PX | Grand Line past 2,500 blocks, the New World, Sabaody, Egghead | From the Sabaody chapter | 260 | Laser volley, laser rain |
| Pacifista Mark III (giant) | New World, Egghead | From Fish-Man Island | 420 | Ground shockwave, laser volley, heavy laser rain |
| Seraphim S-Bear | New World, Egghead | After Wano | 520 | Pad Ho (throws you into the sky), Ursus Shock (big blast) |
| Seraphim S-Hawk | New World, Egghead | After Wano | 480 | Black Blade (long slash line), wing dash |
| Seraphim S-Snake | New World, Egghead | After Wano | 460 | Slave Arrow (turns you to stone for a moment), Perfume Femur |
| Seraphim S-Shark | New World, Egghead | After Wano | 500 | Five Thousand Tile Fist (shockwave), Vagabond Drill |

- Every big move draws a red warning circle first. It turns yellow just before it lands, so step out.
- Below half health a Seraphim lights its Lunarian flame: tougher skin, faster, and fire can't hurt it.
- Run more than 96 blocks away for 30 seconds (or die) and the unit gives up and leaves. Hunts aren't saved: after a restart they're gone.
- Marine players get "Rogue" units with scrambled control chips.

## Parts

| Part | From |
|---|---|
| Pacifista Scrap | Every hunt; ordinary Pacifista (40%) and Seraphim (50%) at Sabaody, Egghead and in raids |
| Pacifista Laser Core | 1 per Pacifista hunt, 2 from a Mark III |
| Vegapunk Control Chip | Pacifista hunts 25%, Mark III 60%, every Seraphim |
| Seraphim Feather | 2-3 per Seraphim hunt, ordinary Seraphim 15% |

## Cyborg modules (Shipwright's Bench)

| Module | Recipe | Tier I / II / III |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Frame Plating | 6 Scrap, 6 Iron Ingots | +2/+4/+6 armour, +1/+2/+3 toughness, III: hard to knock back |
| Strong Right Arm | 6 Scrap, 1 Iron Block, 2 Pistons | +1/+2/+3 attack, +1.5/+3/+4.5 more bare-handed |
| Cola Engine Core | 4 Scrap, 4 Cola, 2 Copper Blocks | Holds 3/6/9 Colas of fuel, faster sprint while fuelled |
| Radical Beam Emitter | 2 Laser Cores, 4 Scrap, 8 Redstone | C: laser beam, 12/16/20 damage, sets on fire, costs 1 Cola |
| Coup de Vent Cannon | 1 Laser Core, 4 Scrap, 4 Wind Charges | Sneak + C: air blast that throws enemies, costs half a Cola; II+: look down to blast yourself up |
| Lunarian Plating | 3 Seraphim Feathers, 4 Scrap, 4 Blaze Powder | Shrug off 1/3, 2/3, all fire; II+: flame armour when badly hurt; III: glide by sneaking in the air |

- Right-click a module to install it. Each module raises one tier. Tier II also uses 1 Vegapunk Chip; tier III uses 2 Chips and 1 Seraphim Feather.
- The Cola Engine refills itself from Colas and Cola Crates in your bag (a crate is 8 Colas). Beams need an engine.
- `/cyborg` shows your upgrades and fuel. Key C can be changed under Options > Controls > "One Piece: Cyborg".

## Operator commands

`/vegapunk hunt <pacifista|mark_iii|s_bear|s_hawk|s_snake|s_shark>` (spawns one 10 blocks away), `/vegapunk here` (which units can find you here), `/vegapunk list`, `/vegapunk stop`, `/cyborg set <player> <upgrade> <tier>`, `/cyborg fill <player>`.
