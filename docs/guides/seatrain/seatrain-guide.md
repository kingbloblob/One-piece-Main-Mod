# Sea Train guide

Ride the Puffing Tom between islands, and build your own Sea Train Stations for fast travel.

## Puffing Tom stops
- Water 7 (the Galley-La dock), Enies Lobby (the Tower of Justice) and every **Sea Train Station** you find on the coasts.
- Walk into a stop once and it's on your timetable for good. You can only ride to stops you've stood at yourself.
- The Puffing Tom takes you once your story **reaches the Water 7 chapter**. Stops you find before that are remembered for later.
- At a stop, click **[Timetable]** in chat (or type `/seatrain`), then **[Ride]**. Ticket: 15 Berries + 1 per 100 blocks.

## Your own Sea Train Station
Craft the **Sea Train Station Blueprint** (paper + blue dye + rail) and the **Sea Train Ticket Booth** (dark oak planks, bell, iron, glass pane, rails). Hold the blueprint to see ghost blocks.
- A 5x5 platform of stone, bricks or planks, with the Ticket Booth in the middle.
- At least 5 rails along one edge of the platform (the track: the train stops on it).
- A fence post 2 tall in each back corner and a 3x5 roof of planks or slabs on top.
- A Bell and 2 lanterns anywhere on the station.

It runs once you've **finished the Water 7 chapter** (Iceburg's crews run your line). Right-click the booth to ride to any stop you know, Puffing Tom stops included. Fare: 1 Coal or Charcoal per 300 blocks. Rename it with `/seatrain name <name>` while standing at it.

## The ride
The train pulls in with you in the cab, blows its horn and steams off; the screen goes dark ("Next stop: ..."), then you pull into the far station. About ten seconds. Sneak to skip it. Crewmates who are following you and pets who aren't sitting come along. You can't board in the middle of a fight. Trains never cross between dimensions.

## Commands
- `/seatrain` timetable at the stop you're at
- `/seatrain list` every stop you know
- `/seatrain name <name>` rename your station
- `/seatrain preview` (operators) park a Puffing Tom next to you for 30 seconds to look at it

Achievements: All Aboard the Puffing Tom (ride once), Station Master (use a station you built).
