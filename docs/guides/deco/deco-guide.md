# Building blocks and plants: player guide

Everything is in the creative tab **One Piece: Building**, and every block, plant and snack can be crafted. The recipe book shows each one once you hold an ingredient.

## How the recipes work

- **Island stone and tiles:** put 8 of a vanilla block around 1 dye or ingredient to get 8. For example, 8 Stone Bricks around White Dye makes 8 Marine Bricks. Some are a 2x2 square instead: 4 Sandstone makes 4 Alabasta Sandstone Bricks, 4 Bricks makes Water 7 Canal Bricks, 4 Snow Blocks makes Snow Bricks, and 4 Island Cloud makes Cloud Bricks.
- **Stairs, slabs and walls:** stairs give 8 (better than vanilla), slabs give 6 and walls give 6. The Stonecutter makes the stone ones one block at a time.
- **Wood sets:** the same recipes as vanilla wood. Saplings can also be crafted: Oak Sapling + Pink Dye makes Sakura, Oak Sapling + Slimeball makes Yarukiman, Jungle Sapling + Sand makes Palm, and Dark Oak Sapling + Black Dye makes Gloom.
- **Flowers:** a vanilla flower plus one extra ingredient gives 2 (for example, Orange Tulip + Blaze Powder makes Fire Lilies). Every flower can also be crafted into dye.
- **Lanterns:** String over Paper over a Torch makes a Paper Lantern. String over Bubble Glass over a Torch makes 2 Bubble Lamps.
- **Storage:** 9 Berries make a Block of Berries, 9 Cola make a Crate of Cola and 9 Mikans make a Crate of Mikans. Each one crafts back. Sake + 2 planks makes a Sake Barrel.

## Where things grow

Sakura trees and petals grow in Wano and Onigashima (and Cherry Groves). Yarukiman trees grow in Sabaody (and Mangrove Swamps), Palms in East Blue, Alabasta, Amazon Lily, Jaya and on normal beaches, and Gloom trees on Thriller Bark. Each island also has its own flowers, such as Cloud Puffs on Skypiea, Fire Lilies on burning Punk Hazard and Lollipop Flowers on Whole Cake. Use an axe on any new log to strip it.
