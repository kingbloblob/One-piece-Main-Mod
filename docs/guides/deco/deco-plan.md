# Building blocks, decorations and plants: the plan

Everything here gets its own texture and a cheap crafting-table recipe (mostly vanilla stuff plus a dye or one plant). Stone blocks can also be made in a vanilla Stonecutter.

## Wood sets (each one has: log, stripped log, wood, planks, stairs, slab, fence, fence gate, door, trapdoor, button, pressure plate, leaves, sapling)

| Wood | Where it grows | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Sakura | Wano | dark bark, pink blossom leaves that drop petals |
| Yarukiman Mangrove | Sabaody | huge pale trees with resin that makes bubbles |
| Palm | East Blue, Alabasta, Amazon Lily, Jaya | tall bent trunk, coconuts |
| Gloom | Thriller Bark | dead grey-purple wood, creepy dark leaves |
| Adam (extras only) | already exists | adds stairs, slab, fence, gate, door, trapdoor to the existing Adam planks |

## Building sets (block + stairs + slab, walls for stone)

- **Alabasta**: sandstone bricks, carved Alabasta tile, Alubarna pillar
- **Wano**: roof tiles (dark blue, great as stairs), red lacquer planks, tatami mat, shoji paper screen (like a glass pane)
- **Marine**: white Marine bricks, blue Marine tiles, Marine pillar
- **Water 7**: canal bricks, Water 7 cobbles
- **Dressrosa**: sunflower mosaic, orange tiles, stucco
- **Fish-Man Island**: coral bricks, pearl block, bubble glass and bubble glass pane
- **Skypiea**: cloud bricks (from Island Cloud), Shandora gold bricks, Shandian ruin stone
- **Drum**: snow bricks, frost stone
- **Punk Hazard**: scorched stone, frozen stone
- **Impel Down / Enies Lobby**: seastone bricks, prison bars
- **Egghead**: lab panel, glowing lab light
- **Whole Cake**: chocolate block, cookie bricks, frosting block, candy cane block

## Decorations

Paper lantern (Wano), bubble lamp (Sabaody), Marine flag, sake barrel, Berry coin pile, cola crate (Franky), mikan crate, tiki torch (Skypiea), dial shell, wanted-poster board.

## Plants

Sakura petals (ground cover), mikan bush (pick mikans like berries), cloud puff and sky lotus (Skypiea), fire lily (Punk Hazard, glows), frost bloom (frozen Punk Hazard), snow lily (Drum), ghost mushroom (Thriller Bark, glows), desert bloom (Alabasta), Amazon jungle flower (tall), giant fern (Little Garden, tall), candy cane sprout and lollipop flower (Whole Cake), pearl coral (Fish-Man Island, never dries out).

## Food and items

Mikan, coconut, meat on the bone, onigiri, dango, sake, Cola bottle, cotton candy, bubble resin (from Yarukiman trees, crafts bubble glass and lamps).

## Putting them in the world

- New trees and flowers spawn in their matching islands (Wano sakura groves, Sabaody mangroves, palm beaches, glowing fire lilies, and so on).
- The existing island buildings get dressed with the new blocks (Wano roofs and lanterns, Marine bricks on Marine bases, mosaic in Dressrosa). This part is coordinated with the islands-and-structures work so nothing gets overwritten.
