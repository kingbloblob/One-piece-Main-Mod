# Fishing, Cooking and the All Blue

## Fishing
Use a normal fishing rod. Most fish you reel in are now the fish of the sea you're in. Type `/fishlog` to see which kinds you've caught.

| Where | Fish |
|---|---|
| East Blue (closer than 1400 blocks to the middle of the world) | Blue Sea Bream, Striped Mackerel, East Blue Lobster |
| Calm Belt (1400 to 1650) | Calm Belt Eel, Sea King Fry (rare), and sometimes a slab of Sea King meat |
| Grand Line (1650 to 3500) | Elephant Honmaguro, Grand Line Puffer (poisonous raw), Rainbow Snapper |
| New World (past 3500) | New World Swordfish, Abyss Anglerfish |
| Fishman Island / Sabaody | Glowfin |
| Skypiea | Sky Fish |
| Wano / Onigashima | Golden Koi |
| Drum Island / frozen Punk Hazard | Frostfin Cod |
| Alabasta | Sandora Catfish |
| Thriller Bark | Ghost Fish |
| The All Blue only | All Blue Kingfish, Rainbow Sunfish |

Any raw fish grills into Grilled Fish in a furnace, smoker or over a campfire.

## The All Blue
One per world, far out in the New World (about 4400 blocks from the middle). Inside it every catch is a fish (junk turns into fish, treasure stays), fish from every sea bite, two fish live nowhere else, and one catch in three brings up a second fish. The water sparkles and is full of tropical fish.

The **All Blue Compass** points the way: make it at the Navigator's Desk from a compass plus a Blue Sea Bream, Calm Belt Eel, Elephant Honmaguro and New World Swordfish (needs a Cook in your crew). Right-click it for the distance.
