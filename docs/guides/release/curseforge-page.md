## Description

**Set sail for the Grand Line!**

This mod turns Minecraft into a full One Piece adventure. Start on a small island near Foosha Village, build your first boat, and work your way from the East Blue to the New World. Progress is slow on purpose: it's meant to last 20 to 50 hours.

### Devil Fruits
- **107 fruits**, each with two moves (R and Sneak + R) and its own passives.
- Logia users dodge attacks, rubber users bounce, and everyone sinks in the sea.
- Find fruits in leaves (very rare) or win them from bosses.
- **Awakening** for every fruit, with famous forms like Gear 5 and Birdcage. It's very hard to unlock.

### Haki and fighting
- Observation, Armament and Conqueror's Haki, trained by using them.
- 10 fighting styles, from Santoryu to Black Leg.
- Guns, swords and custom attack animations.

### Crew, ships and bounties
- Recruit crewmates with jobs, like navigator, cook and doctor.
- Ships with storage and crafting, your own Jolly Roger, cannons, boarding and sea battles.
- Earn a bounty, read about yourself in the newspaper, and get hunted by Marines.
- Become an Emperor and rule your own pirate kingdom.

### The world
- **Grand Line world type:** the sea gets more dangerous the farther you sail, through the East Blue, the Calm Belt, the Grand Line and the New World.
- Island biomes with their own weather, music and climate, like Alabasta, Drum and Skypiea.
- Famous landmarks, busy towns with markets and festivals, caves, Sea Kings, Impel Down and the Holy Land.

### Story and progress
- A story campaign with **52 famous characters**, quest lines and boss fights with several phases.
- Pirate Level stat tree (K), quest tree (J), achievements, and a Crafter's Journal for recipes.
- Endgame: Laugh Tale, the Final War, raids and New Game+.

### Install
1. Install **Fabric Loader** for Minecraft 26.3.
2. Put **Fabric API** and this mod in your `mods` folder.
3. Optional: **JEI** to see recipes, and **Mod Menu** for settings.
4. When you create a world, pick the **Grand Line** world type (it's the default).

### Modpacks
You're welcome to use this mod in modpacks on CurseForge.
