# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (Alpha) - first public release

First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Needs Fabric Loader 0.19.5+ and Fabric API.

- 107 Devil Fruits with two moves each, passives and very hard Awakenings
- Haki (Observation, Armament, Conqueror's) and 10 fighting styles
- 10 playable races and player looks (hair, scars, outfits)
- Story campaign across the Grand Line, 52 famous characters with quest lines, bosses with phases and rematches
- Crew hiring with jobs, ships with storage and crafting, Jolly Roger painter, ship combat and boarding
- Bounties, Marine hunters, raids, Emperor titles and pirate kingdoms
- Grand Line world type: ring seas (East Blue, Calm Belt, Grand Line, New World), 20+ island biomes, famous landmarks, caves, Impel Down and Holy Land
- Busy towns, traders, newspapers and Wanted Posters, weather and island music
- Pirate Level stat tree, quest tree, achievements, HUD and Crafter's Journal
- Guns, melee weapons, crafting stations, ores, deco blocks, plants and 100+ furniture blocks
- Endgame: Laugh Tale, the Final War, raids and New Game+

Known: this is an alpha. Back up your worlds. Report bugs at https://github.com/ryanmTCG/one-piece-mod/issues
