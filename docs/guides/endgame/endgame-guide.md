# Endgame guide

What there is to do after the main story.

## 1. The Laugh Tale finale
Talk to Gol D. Roger at Laugh Tale to finish the story. The finale plays: a title screen, fireworks, and a News Coo headline everyone sees. You also get the **Raid Den Den Mushi**.

## 2. The Final War
One Minecraft day after the finale, the World Government strikes back wherever you are, as long as you're in the Overworld and not on water. You get a warning a minute before it starts. It has 4 waves:
1. A Marine landing party
2. A Pacifista squad led by a Vice Admiral
3. Garp, with Seraphim
4. Admirals Kizaru and Akainu together

## 3. Raids
Hold the **Raid Den Den Mushi**. **Right-click** to pick a crew, and **sneak + right-click** to start that raid where you're standing (on land). Type `/endgame` to see every raid, what unlocks it, and when its treasure is ready again.

| Raid | Waves | Unlocks when you |
|---|---|---|
| The Final War | Marines, Pacifistas, Garp, Kizaru + Akainu | finish the story |
| Beasts Pirates | Gifters, Headliners + King, Kaido | beat Kaido on Hard |
| Big Mom Pirates | Soldiers, Katakuri, Big Mom | beat Big Mom on Hard |
| Blackbeard Pirates | Thugs, captains + Magellan, Blackbeard | beat Blackbeard on Hard |
| Whitebeard's Legacy | Divisions, Marco, Whitebeard | beat Whitebeard on Hard |
| Red Hair Pirates | Crew, sharpshooters, Shanks | win 2 Emperor raids |
| Rocks Pirates | Warriors, Whitebeard + Big Mom, Xebec | beat Xebec, or win 4 Emperor raids |
| Roger Pirates | Oro Jackson crew, Rayleigh, Roger | win all 7 other raids and beat Imu |

- Everyone within 32 blocks joins the raid, and friends who walk up later join too. If you go more than 64 blocks away, you leave the raid.
- More raiders means more enemies, and the bosses take less damage.
- You get the full treasure (Berries, Berry Blocks, enchanted golden apples, netherite, nether stars, stat XP) once every 7 Minecraft days per raid. Repeat wins in between pay 15% of the Berries.
- Your first win of each raid also gives a trophy, like Kaido's Horn or Roger's Captain Coat.
- The raid is lost if everyone falls or runs away for 45 seconds, or after 30 minutes.

## 4. New Game+
After the post-game (beating Imu on the Empty Throne), type `/newgameplus` to see what it does, then `/newgameplus confirm` to start.
- The story starts over at chapter 1, and so do your quests and story events.
- You keep everything else: your fruit, awakening, Haki, stats, styles, crew, ships, titles, kingdoms, bounty and items.
- Bosses near you get 25% more health and 20% more damage for each cycle, plus Resistance from NG+3. Every boss also has at least 1000 + 300 x cycle health and 14 + 2 x cycle damage, so early bosses like Buggy are a real fight again. Their names show `[NG+n]`.
- In raids, New Game+ adds 15% health per cycle (and the usual damage), but no extra Resistance.
- The first time you beat each boss in a cycle, it drops its Devil Fruit again and pays extra Berries. Story boss goals need a new win in the current cycle.
- Raid treasure grows by 50% per cycle. It goes up to NG+5.

## Operator commands (for testing)
`/endgame raid <id>`, `/endgame stop`, `/endgame finale`, `/endgame finalwar`, `/endgame cycle <n>`
