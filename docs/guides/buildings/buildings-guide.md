# Player-built buildings

Every building works like the Pirate Tavern. Craft its **blueprint** (paper + blue dye + one extra item) and its **core block**.

- Right-click the air with the blueprint to read the plan.
- Right-click the ground with the blueprint to see see-through ghost blocks of everything still missing, including the core block. Each ghost vanishes when you place the real block. Sneak and right-click the air to hide them.
- Right-click an unfinished core block to see what is still missing.

| Building | Blueprint extra | Core block recipe | What you build | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Den Den Mushi Relay | copper ingot | copper, redstone, Den Den Mushi, iron | 3x3 stone base, Relay in the middle, 4 fences/walls/bars on top, Lightning Rod on top of those | Right-click to travel to another of your Relays in the same world. Costs 5 Berries, plus 1 for every 200 blocks |
| Galley-La Shipyard | oak boat | logs, iron, Rope Coil, planks | 3x3 plank deck on water (10 of the 16 blocks around it must be water), Crane in the middle, 4-tall log post on the north-west corner with 2 fences sticking east from its top, plus an Anchor, a Rope Coil and a Chest on the deck | Once a day: 16 planks, 4 string, 2 iron. Up to 3 days pile up. A Shipwright in your crew adds Adam Wood |
| Haki Dojo | bamboo | wool, logs, wooden slab | 5x5 floor with 9 or more Tatami, 3-tall log corner posts, 5x5 roof (Wano Roof Tiles count), 4 Shoji Screens, 2 lanterns, Training Post in the middle | Right-click the post: +1 Armament Haki point (2-second cooldown, makes you hungry). Sneak and stand still inside: +1 Observation point every 10 seconds |
| Marine Watchtower | ladder | wooden slabs, iron, spyglass, redstone | four 4-tall legs, 5x5 platform, Alarm Bell in the middle, railing on at least 12 of the 16 edge blocks, a lantern | Marines, bounty hunters and monsters within 48 blocks glow. The bell rings and warns players within 64 blocks when Marines come |
| Baratie Feast Table | bread | carpets, planks, cake | 5x5 wooden floor, table in the middle, 4 wooden stairs as chairs, a campfire or smoker, a cauldron, a lantern | Once a day: uses 8 food from your bag. Everyone within 16 blocks (players and crewmates) gets Strength, Resistance and Regeneration for 5 minutes. With 4 or more kinds of food they also get Speed and Haste. A Cook in your crew makes it last 7.5 minutes |
| Treasure Vault | gold ingot | iron, gold, Berries, chest | 5x5 stone room, 3 tall, with a stone floor and ceiling and a door, a chest and 2 gold, Berry or Shandora Gold blocks inside, Vault block in the middle | Holding Berries, right-click to store them. Sneak and right-click with an empty hand to take out 64. Stored Berries are kept when you die, and they earn 1% a day (at most 100 Berries a day, and at most 7 days count) |

"Stone" means any full stone or brick block, including the mod's Marine Bricks, Seastone Bricks, Canal Bricks and other building blocks.
