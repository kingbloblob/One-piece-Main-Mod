# One Piece Mod (Fabric)

A Minecraft mod that brings One Piece to the game. Built for **Minecraft 26.3** with **Fabric**.

Roadmap: Devil Fruits → Haki → Characters → Ships.

## What's in it so far

### Devil Fruits (107 of them, in the "One Piece" creative tab)

- **Eat** a fruit to get its power for good. It's saved with your world and stays after you die.
- **Press R** to use your fruit's main move. **Sneak (Shift) + R** uses its second move. Each move has a short cooldown.
  You can change the key in Options → Controls → Key Binds → "Devil Fruit".
- **Hover** over a fruit in your inventory to see its type and moves.
- **Finding them:** any leaves block has a 0.05% chance (about 1 in 2,000) to drop a random fruit, and every boss drops its own fruit.
- **The sea:** in water you get weak, sink, and can't use your power. Logia users also lose their dodge in water.
- **Only one:** eating a second Devil Fruit kills you, just like in the anime.

#### Awakening (very hard)

Every fruit can awaken, but it takes a long time:

1. **Mastery 1,000.** Each time one of your fruit's moves hits an enemy, you get 1 mastery. Dying costs you a tenth of it.
   Mastery also makes your moves hit harder: x1 at 0, up to x1.75 at 1,000, and x2 once awakened.
2. **Beat 5 different story bosses** (use their Wanted Posters).
3. **The brink of death.** Once both are done, the next time an enemy would kill you, your fruit awakens instead: full health, lightning, and a message to the whole server.

Press **Y** at any time to see your progress. After awakening, **Y** turns on your awakened form for 20 to 30 seconds (3 minute cooldown).
Each form hits everything around you every second with your fruit's power. Famous fruits have their own forms, like Gomu Gomu's Gear 5,
Doflamingo's Birdcage, Ace's Flame Emperor and Kaido's Thunder Bagua Dragon. Awakening also gives a permanent bonus:
Paramecia moves recharge 25% faster, Logia users dodge 75% of attacks, and Zoan users always regenerate.

Passive powers:
- **Logia** (fire, ice, sand, light, swamp, gas, snow...): half of all attacks pass straight through you, unless the attacker uses Armament Haki.
- **Gomu Gomu:** no fall damage, immune to lightning, and your normal punches send enemies flying.
- **Mera Mera, Magu Magu, Netsu Netsu, Phoenix:** immune to fire. **Hie Hie, Yuki Yuki, Okuchi no Makami:** immune to freezing. **Goro Goro:** immune to lightning. **Bomu Bomu:** immune to explosions. **Doku Doku:** immune to poison.
- **Hie Hie:** the sea freezes under your feet, so you can walk on water.
- **Phoenix:** you slowly heal all the time.
- **Bara Bara, Mochi Mochi, Sube Sube:** about a third of attacks pass through or slide off you.
- **Yomi Yomi, Yamata no Orochi:** come back to life once every 10 minutes.

## First-time setup (do this once)

1. **Install Java 25 (JDK).** Download "Temurin 25 JDK" from https://adoptium.net and install it.
2. **Install IntelliJ IDEA Community Edition** (free) from https://www.jetbrains.com/idea/download.
   You need version 2025.3 or newer.
3. **Open the project.** Unzip this folder, start IntelliJ, choose *Open*, and pick the
   `one-piece-mod` folder (the one with `build.gradle` in it). Click *Trust Project*.
4. **Point Gradle at Java 25.** Go to *Settings → Build, Execution, Deployment → Build Tools → Gradle*
   and set *Gradle JVM* to the Java 25 you installed.
5. **Wait for the import.** The first time, IntelliJ downloads Minecraft and Fabric. This can
   take several minutes. Watch the progress bar at the bottom.

## Run the game with the mod

- In IntelliJ, open the **Gradle** panel on the right, then *Tasks → fabric → runClient*
  and double-click it. Minecraft opens with the mod loaded.
- Or from a terminal in this folder: `./gradlew runClient` (Mac/Linux) or `gradlew runClient` (Windows).

Then make a **Creative** world, open the inventory, find the **One Piece** tab, and eat the fruit.
To test the leaf drop quickly (or to test powers without the creative tab), temporarily raise `DEVIL_FRUIT_LEAF_CHANCE` in `ModLootTables.java` to `1.0f` (100%).
Good tests: jump off a tall pillar (no damage), punch a zombie (it flies), jump into the sea (you sink).

## Make a .jar to play in your normal Minecraft

Run `./gradlew build`. The mod file appears in `build/libs/` (use the one *without* `-sources`).
Copy it into your `.minecraft/mods` folder along with Fabric API, and launch with the Fabric Loader for 26.3.

## Adding another Devil Fruit (try it yourself)

1. In `power/DevilFruits.java`, add a line like the others: id, name, type, two colors and two move names.
   The item, creative tab entry and leaf drop are set up for you automatically.
2. In `power/FruitAbilities.java`, add a `case "your_fruit_id" -> { ... }` to both `primary` and `secondary`.
   Copy a fruit with a similar move and change the numbers.
3. Copy any fruit's JSON files in `assets/onepiece/items/` and `models/item/`, and rename them to your fruit's id.
4. Add a 16×16 PNG with the same name to `textures/item/`.
5. Add `item.onepiece.<id>` (its name) and `fruit.onepiece.<id>.desc` (one line about its power) to `lang/en_us.json`.

## Characters (bosses, quest givers and traders)

All 52 characters from the lore list are in the game. Open the **One Piece: Characters** creative tab.

- **Vivre Card: <name>** places a friendly character. Right-click them: traders open a shop, quest givers give you a quest.
- **Wanted: <name>** places the boss version of that character. Bosses have a health bar, a special attack from their story
  (Enel throws lightning, Whitebeard makes shockwaves, Kizaru teleports...), and drop **Berries** plus their **Devil Fruit**
  when beaten. Every boss's fruit is in the mod, so every boss drops one.
- **Berries** are the money. Bosses drop them, quests pay them, traders take them. Sengoku sells every villain's Wanted Poster,
  so survival players can find bosses to fight.
- **Quest lines:** every quest giver has 3 or 4 steps that follow their story: defeat a boss, bring items, or go talk
  to another character. Right-click them to get a step (a "defeat" step hands you the boss's Wanted Poster), do it,
  right-click them again for the reward, and the next step starts. Good first quest: Koby.
- **Trader levels, like villagers:** every trade gives the trader XP (watch the bar on the trade screen). At Apprentice,
  Journeyman, Expert and Master they unlock new trades, after you close the screen. Trades run out after a few uses
  and restock every Minecraft day.
- Who is a boss, quest giver or trader, and why, is listed in `character-roles.md` next to this zip.

## Pirate crew (hire one crewmate per role)

1. Type `/crew create Red Hair Rookies` (any name). You are now the captain.
2. Type `/crew` to see your crew, the price of your next hire and what each open role asks for.
3. Type `/crew hire cook` (or swordsman, navigator, sniper, doctor, shipwright, archaeologist, musician, helmsman).
   A crewmate appears next to you, follows you, and fights whatever hurts you or whatever you hit.

**It gets harder with every hire.** Each hire costs more Berries than the last
(10, 20, 35, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 300), and each role asks for a trial item.
Dismissing someone doesn't make the next hire cheaper. Late joiners also need a bigger crew first.
In Creative mode hiring is free, so you can test quickly.

| Role | Trial item | Needs crewmates | Perk for the captain (within 48 blocks) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Swordsman | 3 Iron Swords | 0 | Strength I |
| Navigator | 1 Empty Map | 0 | Speed I |
| Sniper (shoots arrows) | 32 Arrows | 0 | Nearby monsters glow |
| Cook (can work a kitchen) | 1 Cake | 0 | Slowly refills hunger |
| Doctor | 2 Golden Apples | 0 | Regeneration below half health, heals the crew |
| Shipwright | 4 Iron Blocks | 3 | Haste I |
| Archaeologist | 8 Books | 4 | Luck I |
| Musician | 1 Jukebox | 5 | Resistance I for you and the crew |
| Helmsman | 1 Heart of the Sea | 6 | Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace |

**Giving orders.** Right-click your crewmate to switch mode: Follow → Wait → Work → Follow.
Work mode makes them stay at that spot and do their job within 10 blocks of it.
Sneak and right-click to open their 9-slot bag, where you can hand them supplies.
Crewmates can't hurt you or each other. If one dies, their role opens up again.
Other commands: `/crew dismiss <role>`, `/crew disband`.

**Jobs in Work mode.** Most jobs use a nearby chest or barrel: crewmates take supplies from it and put what they make or find into it.

| Role | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Swordsman | Guards the spot. Attacks any monster within 16 blocks, then walks back to their post. |
| Sniper | Lookout. Shoots monsters within 24 blocks and warns you in chat ("Enemy spotted!"). |
| Navigator | Tells you when rain, thunderstorms or clear skies arrive. Makes paper (sugar cane), compasses (4 iron + redstone) and empty maps (8 paper + compass). |
| Cook | Farms crops, runs smokers and furnaces, makes bread, pumpkin pie and mushroom stew (see below). |
| Doctor | Infirmary: heals anyone hurt nearby (never monsters) and cures Poison, Wither and Hunger. Makes golden carrots and glistering melon slices from gold. |
| Shipwright | Repairs damaged tools, weapons and armor in their bag or the chest (1 iron ingot = a quarter of the durability). Fixes cracked bricks for free and damaged anvils for 1 iron ingot. |
| Archaeologist | Digs up suspicious sand and gravel. Also studies and finds something every 3 minutes, as fast as every 30 seconds with 15+ bookshelves nearby. Rare finds include diamonds and a Heart of the Sea. |
| Musician | Plays music every few seconds. Nearby crops grow faster and baby animals grow up faster. |
| Helmsman | Put them next to water (10+ water blocks). They fish about every 40 seconds. If you or a crewmate runs low on air nearby, they pull you up. Devil Fruit users get pulled up right away. |

**Setting up a kitchen for your cook.** Build a small area with crops (wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot),
a smoker or furnace, and a chest. Stand the cook in the middle and right-click until they say they're getting to work.
Within 10 blocks of that spot they will:
- harvest fully grown crops and replant them,
- put raw meat, fish, potatoes or kelp into the smoker or furnace, add coal, charcoal, logs or planks as fuel,
  and take the cooked food out,
- make bread (3 wheat), pumpkin pie (pumpkin, sugar, egg) and mushroom stew (bowl, red and brown mushroom) from what they carry,
- put finished food and crops into the chest, and take raw food and fuel out of it when they run low.

Crew code lives in `src/main/java/com/onepiecemod/crew/`. To change prices, edit `HIRE_COSTS` in `CrewHiring.java`;
to change a role's trial item, health, damage, weapon or perk text, edit `CrewRole.java`; perks are in `CrewPerks.java`;
each role's job is in `job/` (for example `job/CookWorkGoal.java`). Outfits are in `assets/onepiece/textures/entity/crewmate/`.

## Ships and Jolly Rogers

Open the **One Piece: Ships** creative tab. In Survival, everything here is made at the **Shipwright's Bench** (see Crafting stations).

**Build a ship any way you like.** Build it in the sea, out of any blocks, any shape. Then:

1. Put a **Ship's Wheel** on the deck. Place it while looking toward the front of your ship: that becomes the bow.
2. Right-click the wheel. Every block connected to it turns into one ship, and you become the captain.
3. **W / S** to sail and slow down, **A / D** to turn.
4. Friends right-click the ship to climb aboard. The first person aboard is always the captain.
5. To dock: stop, then right-click the ship twice. It turns back into normal blocks, lined up with the world,
   and everyone ends up standing on deck. Chests keep their contents.

Good to know:
- **Sails make you faster.** Every wool block counts as a sail, and a painted Sail counts as one per block it covers.
  Big heavy ships need more sails to speed up quickly. With no sails you still move slowly, like rowing.
- Natural blocks (stone, dirt, sand, coral, seagrass...) are never part of a ship, so you can build next to the sea floor.
  The list is in `data/onepiece/tags/block/not_ship.json`. Maximum ship size is 4,096 blocks.
- While sailing, everyone stays at a spot on deck. Pressing sneak hops you off, straight into the sea!
- **Use your ship while sailing.** Look at a block on deck and right-click: chests, barrels, shulker boxes, hoppers and
  ender chests open as normal, and so do crafting tables and the mod's crafting stations (Shipwright's Bench, Ship's Galley...).
  Crewmates share the same chest, and everything is still inside when you dock. Furnaces, brewing stands and doors
  only work once you dock.
- If docking says blocks are in the way, sail into open water and try again.

**Paint your own Jolly Roger.** Hold a **Jolly Roger** and right-click the air to open the paint screen.

- Left mouse paints, right mouse picks up a colour from the picture. Tools: Brush (1 to 4 pixels), Fill bucket, Pick colour.
- **Mirror** paints both halves at once, great for skulls. **Load** starts from the Straw Hat flag, a plain skull and bones, or a blank.
- The checkered colour is see-through: paint with it to cut out any flag shape (pennants, torn edges, round plaques).
- **Detail** sets 16, 32 or 64 pixels across. **Size** sets 1x1, 2x2 or 3x3 blocks.
- **Style**: *Flag* sticks out from a pole and waves. *Sail* hangs from the block facing forward and puffs out in the wind
  (place it on your mast while looking toward the bow). *Plaque* lies flat on a wall like a painting.
- Press **Save**. Right-click a block to hang it up. Break it to get it back, painting included.
- To copy a design, hold another Jolly Roger and right-click a placed one.
- Your flags and sails keep waving while the ship sails.

**Decorations:** Going Merry, Thousand Sunny and Moby Dick figureheads, Ship Cannon, Anchor, Porthole and Rope Coil.
They face the way you look when you place them, so stand on deck facing the sea to put a figurehead on the bow.

## Haki (train it, then switch it on)

Type `/haki` in chat to see your levels and what to do next. Each Haki has levels 1 to 5. Keys can be changed in
*Options → Controls → Key Binds → Haki*.

- **Observation Haki (V key to switch on/off).** Train it by getting hit by mobs. Getting hit while you have
  Blindness counts 5 times (blindfold training, like Koby). Once awakened it is on by default: you get a chance to
  dodge attacks (8% at level 1, 35% at level 5, "Future Sight"), and while you **sneak**, every creature nearby glows
  so you can see it through walls.
- **Armament Haki (B key to switch on/off).** Train it by punching mobs with your bare hands. When on, your hits do
  +2 to +6 damage, you get up to +8 armor from level 2, and your hits land on Logia users. It slowly uses hunger and
  switches off by itself when you are too hungry.
- **Conqueror's Haki (G key to release).** Rare. 1 in 100 players are born with it. Otherwise each boss you beat has a
  2% to 10% chance to awaken it (stronger boss, better odds). Releasing it makes weak hostile mobs nearby faint,
  freezes stronger ones, and staggers other players. Animals and friendly characters are left alone. Cooldown 60s
  at level 1, down to 20s.

### Ores and materials

- **Seastone (Kairoseki)**: ore under every ocean floor, between y=-48 and y=40. Needs an iron pickaxe. Smelt Raw Seastone into Seastone Ingots.
  Devil Fruit users who hold seastone or stand on a Block of Seastone get Weakness, just like in water.
  - **Seastone Jitte** (Smoker's weapon, made at the Seastone Workbench after you beat Smoker): as strong as an iron sword, and drains any Devil Fruit user it hits for 5 seconds.
  - **Seastone Cuffs** (2 ingots and an Iron Chain, at the Seastone Workbench): right-click a Devil Fruit user, player or boss, to seal their power for 10 seconds. Used up after one go.
- **Adam wood**: from the Treasure Tree Adam, a rare giant tree in jungles (common in Skypiea, Elbaph and Little Garden). Much tougher than oak. Perfect for ships.
- **Island Cloud**: the soft white ground of the sky islands.

## Bounties and Marine hunters

Every player has a **bounty**. It starts at 0 and the Marines leave you alone.

- **Beat a boss** (any "Wanted: <name>" boss) and your bounty rises. Beating Buggy adds 15,000,000 Berries,
  Crocodile 70,000,000, Doflamingo 400,000,000, Kaido 1,500,000,000, and so on. Beating the same boss again
  only adds 10% of that. Everyone within 32 blocks when the boss dies gets the bounty, so friends can team up.
- **Beat a Marine** and it rises a little too (1 to 20 million), just like in the anime.
- Your bounty is saved with the world and stays when you die.

**The higher your bounty, the harder the Marines hunt you.** Every 20 seconds there's a chance a squad lands
20 to 32 blocks away and chases you (Overworld only, survival or adventure, never on Peaceful).
After a squad arrives you get a 2 minute break. If you get more than 96 blocks away for a minute, they give up.

| Bounty | Threat level | Who comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Nobody | No one |
| Any | Rookie Pirate | 2 Recruits, now and then |
| 50,000,000 | Known Pirate | 3 Marines with a Rifleman (shoots arrows), more often |
| 100,000,000 | Supernova | 4, led by a Lieutenant. Smoker may lead the hunt |
| 500,000,000 | Worst Generation | 5, led by a Captain. Smoker or CP0's Rob Lucci may come |
| 1,000,000,000 | Emperor Class | 6 with two Captains, very often. An Admiral (Kizaru, Akainu, Fujitora or Ryokugyu) may come |

Famous Marines are the normal boss versions, so beating one raises your bounty and drops their Devil Fruit.
Only one comes at a time, and they leave if you get more than 128 blocks away.
Marines only attack players who have a bounty (unless you hit them first), and hitting one makes the whole squad attack.

**Your Wanted Poster.** When you reach a new threat level, a News Coo tells everyone on the server, and you get
a Wanted Poster with your name and bounty. You also get one the first time you beat each boss.
Right-click a poster to read it.

**Navy raids (bigger than a hunt).** Now and then the Navy raids the spot where you're standing.
A hunt is one small squad that follows you around. A raid stays in one place and comes in **waves**, with a blue raid bar
at the top of the screen, like a village raid. You get a 10 second warning and a horn, and the next wave lands a few seconds
after you beat the last one. Beat every wave to win Berries and a big bounty jump (5,000,000 x waves x threat level).
If you run more than 80 blocks away for 30 seconds the raid ends, but you get nothing. After a raid you get a 10 minute break.

| Threat level | Raid chance each minute | Waves | Warship | Final wave led by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rookie Pirate | 2% (about once an hour) | 2 | No | A Lieutenant |
| Known Pirate | 4% | 3 | Yes, fires every 10 s | A Captain |
| Supernova | 6% | 4 | Yes, every 7 s | Smoker |
| Worst Generation | 9% | 5 | Yes, every 5 s | Rob Lucci or Smoker |
| Emperor Class | 12% | 6 | Yes, every 3 s | An Admiral |

Waves get bigger each time (up to 10 Marines). The **Marine warship** only comes when there's deep sea within about
50 blocks. It anchors offshore and fires cannonballs that arc through the sky and explode near pirates. They hurt, but
they never break blocks. Climb aboard and take the wheel to stop the cannons and steal the ship. If nobody takes it,
it sails away when the raid ends. Marine hunts pause while a raid is on.

**Devil Fruit bounty hunters.** From a bounty of 100,000,000, bounty hunters come for you too. They aren't Marines.
They want the Berries on your head, and **every hunter has a Devil Fruit**. They use its moves every few seconds, the same moves
the story bosses use: a Mera Mera hunter throws fire like Ace, a Pika Pika hunter teleports next to you like Kizaru.
Like every fruit user they go weak in water, so knocking them into the sea helps.

| Threat level | Hunters at once | Health | Fruits they can have | Chance every 30 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supernova (100M) | 1 | 30 hearts | Fruits of East Blue to Paradise villains (Buggy, Crocodile, Enel...) | 4% |
| Worst Generation (500M) | 1 or 2 | 50 hearts | Also New World fruits (Doflamingo, Law, Katakuri...) | 6% |
| Emperor Class (1B) | 2 or 3 | 75 hearts | Any fruit, even an Admiral's or an Emperor's | 10% |

After hunters come you get a 5 minute break, and none come during a Navy raid. Beating one raises your bounty
(10 to 60 million) and drops Berries, with a 1 in 10 chance of dropping their Devil Fruit. If you get more than
96 blocks away for 90 seconds, they give up.

**Commands**

## The seas: Sea Kings, the Calm Belt, Log Poses and Den Den Mushi

Everything here is in the **One Piece: Seas** creative tab.

**The seas of the world.** The Overworld's oceans are split into stripes along the Z axis (press F3 to see your Z):
the **Grand Line** runs through the middle (Z between -1000 and 1000), a **Calm Belt** lies on each side of it
(Z from 1000 to 1400, and from -1000 to -1400), and the **Blue seas** are further out. A message pops up when you sail into a new sea.
In the Calm Belt there is **no wind**: ship sails do nothing there and ships only crawl along. It's also where Sea Kings nest.

**Sea Kings** are giant sea serpents (about 12 blocks long) with a boss bar. They hunt anyone they can see out at sea,
**knock you off your boat or ship** into the water, and can't follow you onto land (they slowly dry out if stranded).
How often one shows up near you, checked every 10 seconds: very often in the Calm Belt (up to 3 at once),
sometimes in deep Grand Line ocean, rarely in deep Blue sea ocean, never on Peaceful.
They drop 3 to 6 **Raw Sea King Meat**, Sea King Scales and some Berries. When a player kills one you also get 1 or 2 pieces of treasure from its belly: an enchanted book, Sea Pearls, Prismarine, Nautilus Shells, gold, diamonds, a rare enchanted Trident or a very rare Heart of the Sea. About 1 Sea King in 67 has swallowed a random **Devil Fruit** (change `DEVIL_FRUIT_CHANCE` in `sea/SeaKingLoot.java`). Cook the meat (furnace, smoker or campfire) into **Sea King Meat on the Bone**,
which fills 6 hunger drumsticks.

**Log Pose** (a compass on a wrist strap). Keep it in your inventory and stand on an island for **15 seconds**:
it records that island and the needle swings to point at the **next island**. Sail there, wait 15 seconds again, and it points to the one after.
It never points back to the last 16 islands you recorded. Until it's set, the needle spins. Right-click it to read where it points and how far.

**Eternal Pose** (a Log Pose in a wooden frame). A new one is blank. Right-click it while standing on an island and it points
to that island **forever**, so you can always find your way home. Make one for each island you want to get back to.

**Den Den Mushi** (snail phone). *Puru puru puru... gacha!* Right-click to call your crew: every crewmate who can hear you
appears next to you and follows you. Sneak and right-click to tell them all to wait where they are.
Crewmates can only hear you if they're in the same dimension and their area is loaded (not too far from a player).

**Sea King Bait.** Right-click while facing deep water (at least 8 blocks deep) and a Sea King rises 12 blocks in front of you.
Good for testing and for meat hunting.

## Crafting stations (slow progression)

The mod's gear is no longer made at a normal crafting table. Instead there are five **crafting stations**, and
each one opens up the next. Right-click a station to see everything it can make:

- **White** = you can craft it now. **Yellow** = you're missing ingredients. **Red** = locked: the right side says what
  you still have to do (beat a boss, raise your bounty, record islands, train Haki).
- Station recipes don't use a grid. Just carry the ingredients in your inventory and press **Craft**.
- They're in the **One Piece: Crafting** creative tab.

| Tier | Station | How to build it (crafting table) | What it makes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 East Blue | **Shipwright's Bench** | Iron Axe on top, Planks + Crafting Table + Planks, Logs in the corners below | Ship's Wheel, Rope, Jolly Roger, Porthole, Anchor, Cannon (beat **Buggy**), figureheads: Merry, Moby (**100M** bounty), Sunny (Adam Planks, beat **Lucci**) |
| 1 East Blue | **Ship's Galley** | 3 Iron, Iron + Smoker + Iron, 3 Bricks | Sea King Bait, **Pirate Bento**, **Sea King Stew**, **Rumble Ball** |
| 2 Grand Line | **Navigator's Desk** | Paper, Map, Paper / Gold, Cartography Table, Gold / Ship's Wheel below | Log Pose, Den Den Mushi, Eternal Pose (record **3 islands** first) |
| 3 Seastone | **Seastone Workbench** | 3 Seastone Ingots, Iron + Smithing Table + Iron, Iron in the bottom corners | Seastone Cuffs, Seastone Jitte (beat **Smoker**) |
| 4 Meito | **Swordsmith's Forge** | Diamond + Seastone Jitte + Diamond, Deepslate Bricks + Blast Furnace + Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks + Anvil + Deepslate Bricks | Sandai Kitetsu (**30M** bounty), Wado Ichimonji (**100M** bounty and Armament Haki level 1) |

The chain: the Navigator's Desk needs a Ship's Wheel (from the Shipwright's Bench), the Seastone Workbench needs Seastone
from the ocean floor, and the Forge needs a Seastone Jitte, which you can only make after beating Smoker.
Shusui, Enma and Yoru are still boss drops only.

New items:

- **Pirate Bento**: Sanji's packed lunch. Fills 7 hunger shanks.
- **Sea King Stew**: a hearty stew you can eat even when full (makes 2).
- **Rumble Ball**: Chopper's pill. 30 seconds of Strength II, Speed and Resistance. Taking a second one while
  the first is still working makes you sick (Nausea, Weakness, Slowness).

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
