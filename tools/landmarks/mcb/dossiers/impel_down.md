# Impel Down (structure id impel_down) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Impel Down arc (ch. 525-549), the old v1 build. The Nether has separate
Impel Down hell structures (blazing_hell_furnace with Magellan, freezing_hell_cells, eternal_hell_vault, newkama_land).

## Recognisable features
A great prison fortress rising out of the Calm Belt sea: round walls, a big central tower, a sea gate where Marine
ships dock. Below the sea, six levels going down: Crimson Hell (blade forest), Wild Beast Hell, Starvation Hell
(heat, sand), Blazing Hell (boiling cauldron), Freezing Hell (ice) with secret Newkama Land, Eternal Hell (Lv6).

## Scale (site ~105 x 110)
Outer drum r50 (old 34), six levels 11 high down to y -82, wall to y 18, gate towers ~45, tower to ~84, flag ~96.

## Palette roles
onepiece:seastone_bricks + polished deepslate; prison_bars; each level's own floor/wall (crimson nylium + red nether
bricks, moss + mossy stone, sand + sandstone, blackstone, snow + packed ice, obsidian).

## v2 additions
36 cells per level with bunks, chains and buckets, an upper gallery, a furnished guard station on every level, the
lava cauldron with bridges, beast pens, dry wells, frozen prisoners, solitary obsidian cells on Lv6, Newkama Land's
party hall, a walled stair through all levels, a furnished tower (offices, records, lookout), barracks, armory and
infirmary of two floors in the courtyard, gate towers with ladders.
