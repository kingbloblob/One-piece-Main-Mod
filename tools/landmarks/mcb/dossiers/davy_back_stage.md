# Long Ring Long Land / Davy Back Fight - dossier (batch 10)
Sources: WebSearch snippets (One Piece wiki Long Ring Long Land Arc, recaps of the Foxy Pirates arc).
- Long Ring Long Land: a ring of long, thin, flat grass islands joined at low tide; everything (trees, animals) is stretched long.
- Davy Back Fight: Foxy's games where crews wager members. Rounds: Donut Race (boat race round the island), Groggy Ring (ball game with a goal ring), Combat (one-on-one, in a ring).
- Foxy Pirates set up a festival: grandstands, food and game stalls, tents; Foxy's ship the Sexy Foxy has a fox figurehead.
Design brief: 176 site, ground y 2. Flat open lawn r 30 in the middle (arena thread needs it: buoys at 28) with flush white pitch lines only; goal rings on poles just outside it; grandstands N/S with striped awnings; Foxy's striped command tent (stage, throne, party hall, rooms) west; Combat boxing ring with string lights; midway stalls + bunting; crew tents; pier with start gate + barrel boats; Sexy Foxy (shipkit, mcb/sexy_foxy.py) moored south-east; tall thin "long" trees.
