# Sea King Graveyard
Refs: Sea Kings of the Calm Belt (ep 71 / ch 101), giant Sea Kings round Fish-Man Island (ch 608+): serpents with horned, fanged heads.
Silhouette: 150-block S spine diving in and out of the sand, rib pairs arching up like cathedral vaults (tallest near the head), dorsal crest spines, a huge horned skull on its side with jaws gaping and fang rows, bone fins on the sand.
v2: 160x112 seafloor, max ~25 above the floor (fits a normal ocean). Coral/sea pickles on bone tops, kelp at the edges, fish school through the ribs, gold in the mouth, glowing eye sockets.
