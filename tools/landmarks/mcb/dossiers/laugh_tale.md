# Laugh Tale (structure id laugh_tale) dossier
Depth: quick (canon shows very little of the island). Scale: site 176 x 176, temple 58 across.

## Canon (manga ch. 967-968, wiki summaries)
- The final island of the Grand Line; Roger named it after the joke of Joy Boy's treasure (the crew burst out laughing).
- Little is shown: a shore of steep cliffs and a lot of wilderness, with shapes like buildings or fortifications on it.
- Only the Roger Pirates reached it (25 years before the story). Joy Boy left the treasure there 800 years ago.

## Design choices (not shown in canon, flagged)
- A cove on the south side (the only landing), a ridge of steep sea cliffs round the north and west (the cliffs seen
  from the sea), jungle wilderness swallowing the ruins of an ancient city of the Void Century.
- The Sun Gate of Joy Boy on the avenue from the cove, two ring walls of ancient stone round the temple.
- The stepped temple in the middle: entrance hall of murals (ancient_glyph_stone), the Poneglyph chamber (west), the
  Hall of Joy Boy (east), and the treasure room at the heart: a big gold pile (gold blocks, raw gold, berry blocks,
  treasure_pile props, gold plates as coins) with onepiece:joy_boy_chest[treasure=true] on top, facing the entrance.
- Roger (npc, not a boss) waits in front of the pile on a 9 x 9 clear floor, 12 blocks from the chest (finale finds the
  chest within 16 sideways / 8 up of the player, Finale.findChest). Peaceful inside: no guards in the temple.

## Palette roles
base: stone_bricks / mossy / cracked mix; trim: chiseled stone bricks, polished andesite; accent: gold_block, shandora
gold bricks, ancient_glyph_stone murals; floors: polished deepslate + gold inlay in the treasure room.

## Ground (ryan's batch 4 rules)
Irregular outline, relief fades to the beach before the site edge, ground cover in noise patches (grass, moss, podzol,
rooted dirt, coarse dirt, mud), plants in drifts. No ship (the old canvas ship looked basic).
