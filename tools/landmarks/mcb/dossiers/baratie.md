# Baratie dossier
Depth: standard (web pages blocked in the cloud box; snippets + ryan's show still previews/references/baratie-reference.png)
Scale: 1 block = 1 m, ~125 blocks tail to lips, 34 wide, roof ridge y 47, mast flags y ~82.
## Identity
- Zeff's sea restaurant, East Blue (Baratie arc). Ship + building hybrid: a round hull with a big fish head at the bow.
- Recognised by: fish head with thick orange lips, mint-green stepped building, red-orange roof, two masts with yellow/white swag sails and red BT flags.

## Layout
Hull oval in plan. 4 levels (wiki): dining hall (ground), kitchen (middle), cooks' quarters (top) + hold below deck.
Stepped tiers, each step is a purple-railed balcony. Masts stand on deck at both building ends, rope ladders to the tops.
Fins fold out from under the hull (stabilisers / battle platforms) -> small folded fins at the waterline.

## Palette roles
base/hull: mangrove planks above water, dark oak below; white strake at deck edge
wall: waxed oxidized copper family (mint) mixed with prismarine bricks for texture
trim: smooth quartz pilasters/band, bamboo planks (yellow) window frames
roof: onepiece:dressrosa_tiles (+stairs/slab)
accent: purpur posts + purple glass rails, orange/yellow concrete fish parts, red BT flags
## Unknowns / design choices
- Back (starboard) side not in the reference: mirrored (ship is symmetric in the show).
- Interior layout: design choice from the wiki floor list; fish-head bar inside the head is from the LEGO set, not canon.
- Cannons in the fish mouth (Bandai model note): 3 onepiece:cannon blocks inside the mouth.
