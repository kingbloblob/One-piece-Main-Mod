# Thriller Bark (structure id thriller_bark) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Thriller Bark arc (ch. 442-489), the old v1 build (tools/landmarks/thriller_bark.py).

## Recognisable features
- The world's largest pirate ship: a colossal dark hull carrying a whole haunted island, wrapped in permanent fog,
  drifting in the Florian Triangle. From far away you see the hull, a wall, the great mast with tattered sails and
  Moria's bat-winged Jolly Roger, and the spiky castle at the stern.
- On the island, bow to stern: the bow gate, the misty forest of twisted trees, the graveyard (tombstones, crosses,
  open graves; zombies climb out), Absalom's wedding chapel, Hogback's mansion (his lab and operating rooms where
  the zombies were made, the freezer for Cindry), Perona's garden, the zombie barracks, and Moria's castle with
  the throne hall where the giant Moria sat, the dining hall and Perona's pink room.

## Scale (site ~224 x 112, cap 116 reach)
Hull x 4..219, beam 100, keel -14, deck 12. Island wall to y 22, castle 48 x 68 with 4 floors (7 high) and a 20-high
throne hall, corner towers with 20-high spires to ~y 85, mast to y 124 with the flag, bow gate 28 high with two
spired towers.

## Palette roles
hull: dark oak / mangrove planks, stripped dark oak trim; wall: deepslate bricks + cracked + polished blackstone;
roofs: deepslate tiles; spires: purple terracotta; glass: purple panes; ground: onepiece:gloom_grass, gloom logs
and leaves; accents: soul lanterns, candles, pumpkins, cobwebs, pink for Perona.

## v2 additions
A walkable hold deck of storerooms and zombie cells, gun ports and a lit stern gallery, furnished rooms on every
castle floor, the giant throne, Perona's room, Hogback's lab/operating room/freezer, Absalom's chapel with pews and a
bell spire, a mausoleum with crypts, zombie barracks, Perona's garden and gazebo, crow's nest on the mast.
