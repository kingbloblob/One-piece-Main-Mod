# Dressrosa Royal Palace (structure id dressrosa_palace) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Dressrosa arc (ch. 700-801), the old v1 build.

## Recognisable features
- The King's Plateau: a sheer natural rock mesa in the middle of the flower town; a long stair on its face.
- The royal palace on top: pink and white, round towers with tiled cone roofs, a tall central keep; the Donquixote
  Jolly Roger (a smiley crossed out with a slash) on Doflamingo's flag.
- The flower town: pastel stucco houses, orange tiled roofs, cafes, flowers, sunflower fields, fountains, mosaic
  streets. During the arc, the Birdcage: Doflamingo's strings form a dome cage over the whole country.

## Scale (site 172 x 172)
Plateau 76 x 72, top y 32; palace 56 x 40, 3 floors 8 high (two-storey throne hall), corner towers r6 with 18-high
cones, keep r8 to ~y 110 with the flag; birdcage apex y 140, radius 82.

## Palette roles
plateau: stone/andesite/tuff bands, ivy, azalea; palace: onepiece:dressrosa_stucco + pink terracotta, white
terracotta trim, quartz stair/fountain; roofs: onepiece:dressrosa_tiles; streets: dressrosa_mosaic + tiles;
accent: striped wool awnings, flowers, sunflowers, gold.

## v2 additions
Two-storey throne hall with a dais and throne, quartz columns and chandeliers, furnished wings on 3 floors,
Doflamingo's pink suite, enterable towers and keep, a garden courtyard with fountain and hedges, ~50 furnished town
houses (4 per street block) with awnings, fountains, lamp posts.
