# Punk Hazard (structure id punk_hazard_lab) dossier
Depth: quick (anime/manga memory of the Punk Hazard arc, ch. 655-699; web pages blocked).
Scale: site 176 x 168, main lab 61 x 37 x 3 floors.

## Canon
- The island is split in two after Kizaru and Aokiji's duel: one half burning (lava, scorched rock), one half frozen.
- Caesar Clown's research facility (the old Government lab Vegapunk used) in the middle: the SAD production room,
  the Biscuit Room where the kidnapped children lived, Caesar's office, gas tanks and the Shinokuni gas.
- The G-5 Marines (Smoker, Tashigi) land on the frozen side; Law has been on the island; the dragon Law cut down.

## Design choices (flagged)
- Frozen half west, burning half east, a rocky spine along the line where they meet, steam/mud along the line.
- Main building: white lab with cyan bands, a two-storey entrance hall and SAD chamber (purple vat), wings of labs and
  stores, Caesar's office and the control room under a glass-ribbed dome on top.
- Biscuit Room building west (pink trim, beds, ball pit, slide, sweets table), joined by a glass tube.
- Gas works east with three tanks and a spherical reservoir (not canon shapes, chosen for the read).
- The burnt-out old lab and the dragon's bones on the burning half (bones are a choice: canon shows the dragon cut up).
- The G-5 Marine warship (real ship template) frozen into a western cove.

## Ground (ryan's batch 4 rules)
Irregular island fading to its beaches; snow/ice and ash/basalt cover in noise patches; snow layers in drifts;
lava only in rimmed pools.
