# Shift Station (sea_train_station)
Refs: ch. 322, ep. 228-229 (Kokoro, Chimney, Gonbe). Wiki only says "small stop in the middle of the ocean"; shape from the anime.
Silhouette: small grassy island, a brick-and-timber station house with a clock tower, rails running into the sea just under the surface, the line forking (the "shift") at a signal cabin on stilts.
Puffing Tom: steam engine with big red paddle wheels each side, tall flared stack, round smokebox face with a lamp, tender + carriages.
v2 (2026-10-30): 144x112, station house 2 floors + clock tower, island platform with striped canopy and "SHIFT STATION" board, Kokoro's house-bar, engine shed with pit, coal stage, water tower, ~6 packed cottages (mcb/lotpack.py), jetty with rowboats.
