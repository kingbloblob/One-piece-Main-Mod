# Enies Lobby (structure id tower_of_justice) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Enies Lobby arc (ch. 375-430), the old v1 build (tools/landmarks/enies_lobby.py),
landmark-show-accuracy notes (Gates of Justice were too short).

## Recognisable features
- The judicial island of the World Government sits in a vast hole in the sea: the ocean pours over the rim on every
  side, so the islands stand on rock pillars in a foaming abyss. It is always daytime there.
- South to north: the Main Gate (huge gatehouse with towers) -> the town where the officials live -> the Courthouse
  (wide classical white building, colonnade, clock tower; the giant three-headed judge Baskerville) -> the
  drawbridge over the chasm -> the Tower of Justice on its own rock (tall white stepped tower, CP9's base; the WG
  flag on its roof that Sogeking shot) -> the Bridge of Hesitation -> the Gates of Justice (colossal grey gates).
- CP9 members' rooms in the tower each had a character: Blueno's bar, Kalifa's bath, Kumadori's tatami room,
  Jabra's den, Kaku's room, Spandam's office with the golden Den Den Mushi on top.

## Scale (site 224 x 224, cap 116 reach)
Main island ellipse 208 x 120 (south), courthouse 85 x 37 with a 46 high clock dome; tower rock 72 x 52; Tower of
Justice 31 square stepping to 13, roof at y 84, flag to ~108; Gates of Justice 66 wide, 104 tall on a north rock.
Abyss floor y -40 with foam; islands are rock pillars from -40 to 0.

## Palette roles
base: stone/andesite rock pillars, stone brick plinths; wall: onepiece:marine_bricks + white concrete; trim:
marine_pillar, tile cornices; roofs: deepslate tiles (dark slate); accent: blue WG flags, gold clock, lanterns;
gates: polished deepslate + deepslate tiles, white emblem.

## v2 additions
Furnished floors in every tier of the tower (ring corridor round a stair core), CP9 character rooms, Spandam's
office, a courtroom with Baskerville's high bench and gallery, courthouse offices, furnished town houses, guard
rooms in the gatehouse, a walkable Bridge of Hesitation, taller show-accurate Gates of Justice.
