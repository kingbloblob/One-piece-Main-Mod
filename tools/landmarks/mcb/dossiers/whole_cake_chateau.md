# Whole Cake Chateau (structure id whole_cake_chateau) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Whole Cake Island arc (ch. 825-902), the old v1 build and the show-accuracy review.

## Recognisable features
Big Mom's castle shaped like a giant multi-layer cake with frosting drips, filling stripes, turrets with candy
spires, strawberries and candles, at the centre of Sweet City (candy and cake houses, lollipop trees), the Seducing
Woods round it. Inside: the grand hall, Big Mom's throne room, the treasure room with the Road Poneglyph.

## Scale
Site 210 x 216 (at the cap); cake r40 at the base, ~106 tall. v2 keeps the castle and rebuilds Sweet City.

## Palette roles
onepiece:chocolate_block, cookie_bricks, frosting_block/stairs, candy_cane_block; pink/white/yellow/light blue
terracotta and concrete; cherry wood inside; lollipop flowers, frost blooms, candy cane sprouts.

## v2 additions
60 furnished cake houses in two rings (2-3 floors, ladders, chimneys of candy cane, frosting/chocolate/cookie/cherry
roofs), doors facing the castle, an open avenue to the gate, candy flowers between them.
