# Little Garden - dossier (batch 12)
Sources: series knowledge (Little Garden arc ep. 70-77), fandom wiki (Brogy page: Sea King skeleton where Dorry pinned Luffy).
- Prehistoric jungle island; giant trees, dinosaurs, giant ferns; central volcano whose eruption starts each round of the 100-year duel between Dorry (sword, round shield, horned helm) and Brogy (axe, shield, red horned helm).
- Dorry's camp: giant-sized hut/tent of hides, bonfire with a roasting dinosaur, barrels of booze; Brogy's camp near the giant Sea King skeleton.
- Mr. 3's Candle House (white wax hut) and the Candle Service Set (a cake-shaped wax platform with a pumpkin-like top on a spiral) in the jungle.
Design brief: 160 site land; volcano in the north with crater glow and smoke; duel ground (flat clearing) in the middle with craters; Dorry west, Brogy east, both giant scale (furniture x4); Sea King skeleton (ribcage + skull) by Brogy; dinosaur skeleton; Candle House + Candle Service Set south; giant jungle trees, ferns, river to the sea; chests; baroque_agent guards near the wax house.
