# Orange Town / Buggy's Big Top - dossier (batch 10)
Sources: WebSearch snippets (One Piece wiki Chapter 16, Chouchou/Boodle pages via breezewiki, recaps).
- Buggy's crew took over Orange Town; townsfolk fled. Buggy sat on a rooftop (the tavern) with his flag; the Buggy Ball cannon blasted a lane of houses (one shot hit Mayor Boodle's house).
- Chouchou the dog guards the pet food shop of his late owner.
- Buggy's ship is the Big Top (template big_top exists). Circus theme: striped tent, cages, trapeze.
Design brief: 168 site, ground y 2, harbour south. Stucco houses with orange Dressrosa tile roofs (house_module), tavern HQ (3 storeys, bar, crew rooms, flat front terrace with Buggy Ball cannon aimed down the town, Buggy's chair + parasol, cage, flag, BUGGY sign), scorched blast lane with smashed houses and crater, the striped Big Top in the square (ring, bleachers, trapeze, cages), pet food shop with kennel, Big Top ship at the quay.
