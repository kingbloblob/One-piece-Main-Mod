# Alubarna Palace (structure id alubarna_palace) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Alabasta arc (ch. 155-217), the old v1 build.

## Recognisable features
- Alabasta's royal palace stands on a high flat-topped rock above the capital Alubarna: sandstone walls with round
  corner towers and bulb domes, a great central dome over the throne room, minarets, palm gardens with pools.
- Below the plateau the city: flat-roofed sandstone houses with awnings, markets, and the clock tower in the plaza
  (where Pell carried the bomb). Under the palace is the royal tomb with the Poneglyph Robin read.

## Scale (site 224 x 224, cap 116 reach)
Mesa 196 x 114, plateau at y 24; palace 96 x 56, 3 floors 7 high; great dome r15; clock tower ~50 high in the city.

## Palette roles
base: sandstone/smooth sandstone, cut sandstone trim; roofs: flat with parapets; domes: white/quartz with gold
bands; accent: terracotta stripes, cyan glass, palms, blue pools, striped wool awnings.

## v2 additions
Throne room under the great dome, furnished wings on 3 floors, wing-roof onion domes, a hidden stair down to the
tomb with sarcophagi and the onepiece:poneglyph, barracks, guest house, a market plaza with stalls and dense
furnished flat-roof houses, the clock tower with its bell.
