# Shandora / Upper Yard (structure id shandora) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Skypiea arc (ch. 237-302), the old v1 build.

## Recognisable features
- Upper Yard: solid land floating on clouds, giant jungle trees over the ruins of Shandora (grey-tan carved stone
  with gold), stepped Mesoamerican buildings and a great temple.
- Giant Jack, the twisted beanstalk through the city; the Upper Ruins on a cloud partway up; the Golden Belfry
  with the great bell at the top. The sacrificial altar at the edge where the Going Merry was left; Enel's golden
  ark Maxim built in Upper Yard.

## Scale (site 180, v1 124; floats at world y 162)
Island r ~84 with a cloud underside; temple 45 wide, 5 tiers of 6 to a gold top shrine (~y 41); Jack to y 82.

## Palette roles
onepiece:shandian_ruin_stone family, shandora_gold_bricks, moss; island_cloud / cloud_puff; jungle logs/leaves.

## v2 additions
~23 ruined two-storey buildings (broken tops, fallen floors, furnished ground floors, ladders) and stepped
shrines, temple hall with pillars, vault, shrine and scroll rooms, sacrificial altar with obelisks, Ark Maxim on
its cradle, ~22 giant trees.
