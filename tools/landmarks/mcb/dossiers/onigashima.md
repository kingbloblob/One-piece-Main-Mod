# Onigashima (structure id onigashima) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Onigashima raid (Wano act 3, ch. 980-1049), the old v1 build and the 2026-10-25
show-accuracy review (landmark-show-accuracy), which already matched the skull face, horns, rooftop and castle.

## Recognisable features
Giant horned skull dome on a rocky island: brow, black eye sockets, heart nose, row of teeth with the gate, two horns,
flat rooftop. Inside: ground hall with War Beast pens, Live Floor with buildings lining both walls, Kaido's
multi-storey castle against the back wall with the Road Poneglyph, rooftop doors.

## Scale
Site 220 x 220 (at the cap), skull ~92 high, horns ~112. v2 keeps the shell (reach cap) and redoes the insides.

## v2 additions
Castle storeys 2, 4 and 5 split into bsp rooms (barracks, stores, library, living, bedrooms) with lanterns, the
climbing ladder kept clear; all six Live Floor buildings furnished on both floors with ladders; Gifter bunk rooms,
workshop and stores built along the back of the ground hall.

## v4 (2026-10-30, ryan: "darker, on a floating island with an upside-down jagged cone as the bottom")
References: ryan's manga panel (island lifted out of the sea, horned skull on top, a ragged rock cone hanging under
it with chunks breaking off), the anime frame "Kaido is lifting Onigashima itself!" (black skull with lit eyes over a
dark night sea, purple sky) and the festival frame (glowing skull eyes over a lantern-lit town, a pond with lily pads,
an arched bridge, a red pavilion, sakura and snowy pines, a giant katana).
Silhouette: island top 78 above the sea, ~190 wide; under it a jagged cone narrowing to a point ~6 above the water
(straight-ish sides, 300 hanging spikes, a spike cluster at the tip). Skull 92 high, horns to ~112, castle on top.
Palette: skull smooth basalt (one clean skin), tuff ridges, polished tuff teeth, shroomlight eyes and nose; horns
polished blackstone -> blackstone -> deepslate tiles; cone deepslate/cobbled deepslate/blackstone/basalt strata;
castle walls black terracotta; ground podzol/coarse dirt/rooted dirt with snow patches.
Access: a dock on the sea under the island, a wooden stair tower to a lantern-lit stair cut into the cone, arriving at
the front of the island at the torii avenue to the skull's gate.
