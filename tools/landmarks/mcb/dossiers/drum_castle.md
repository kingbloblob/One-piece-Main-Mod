# Drum Castle (structure id drum_castle) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Drum Island arc (ch. 132-154), the old v1 build.

## Recognisable features
- The Drum Rockies: sheer, flat-topped cylindrical rock columns covered in snow; the castle sits on the tallest.
- Drum Castle: a white/snowy European castle with blue conical tower roofs and a central keep with a pale onion
  dome; Hiluluk's Jolly Roger (skull, crossbones, sakura) raised on top; cannons along the summit that fire the pink
  powder (the Sakura of Hiluluk), pink snow.
- The ropeway from the summit to the foot; the town of Bighorn below (Dalton's town), snowy timber houses.
- Kureha's clinic inside the castle, Chopper's room, the library, the old king Wapol's armory.

## Scale (site 150 x 150)
Main column r 27, summit y 74; castle 38 x 24, 3 floors 7 high, corner towers r5 with 14-high cones, keep r7 with
an onion dome r8 to ~y 130 and the flag to ~140. Lower peaks 50/40/34 high.

## Palette roles
rock: stone/andesite strata with diorite bands, snow ledges, packed ice icicles; castle: onepiece:snow_bricks with
stone brick pilasters; roofs: deepslate tiles, blue terracotta cones, light blue terracotta dome; accent: pink
concrete powder snow, pink petals, red carpets, spruce.

## v2 additions
3 furnished floors with a great hall, clinic, lab, kitchen, library and bedrooms; enterable towers with ladders; the
keep room; a hidden stair winding up inside the rock from a tunnel at the foot; ropeway gondola; Bighorn with 14
furnished houses, a plaza with a well, paths and a spruce forest.
