# Moby Dick (structure id moby_dick) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory (Whitebeard Pirates, ch. 434 flashbacks, Marineford arc), the old v1 build.

## Recognisable features
- Whitebeard's flagship: a huge white ship whose bow is a giant white sperm-whale head with a smiling mouth and
  small eyes; red paddle wheels on both sides amidships; several masts with square white sails, the main ones
  painted with Whitebeard's skull-and-crescent-moustache mark; his Jolly Roger at the main top.
- On deck Whitebeard sits in his giant chair with his bisento; the crew party at long tables with sake.
- 16 divisions live aboard (quarters), plus the nurses (infirmary), mess hall, galley.

## Scale (site ~220 x 70, cap 116 reach)
Hull x 4..186 + whale head to x 220, beam 56 (half 28), keel -14, hold -10, gun decks -2 and 5, main deck 13;
whale head r 26 x 21 x 25 up to y 29 with a lookout deck; masts 58-92 high above the deck.

## Palette roles
hull: white concrete, light/gray concrete low; trim: stripped dark oak wales; decks: birch planks, dark oak inside;
accent: red terracotta paddle wheels and chair, gold, white wool sails with black mark, lanterns.

## v2 additions
Hold with treasure room + stores, lower gun deck with division quarters between cannon rows, upper gun deck with
a mess hall, galley, infirmary, armory; whale-head lounge with 2 store decks and a lookout on the crown; 3-tier
stern castle with furnished rooms, Whitebeard's giant bed, chart room, helm.
