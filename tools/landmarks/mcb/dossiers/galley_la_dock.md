# Water 7 + Galley-La Dock 1 (structure id galley_la_dock) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Water 7 arc (ch. 323-374), old v1 build (tools/landmarks/water7.py),
landmark-show-accuracy notes (Galley-La HQ was weak).

## Recognisable features
- The Water Capital: a round city that rises in terraces to the Great Fountain on top; water from the fountain feeds
  the canals that ring every level. Yagara bulls pull boats along the canals; bridges cross them.
- Tall narrow Italian/Venetian houses: pale stucco, sandstone or brick, red tile roofs, many windows.
- Galley-La Company on the shore: Dock 1 with slipways, giant cranes, ships on the ways and hulls in their ribs;
  the headquarters (Iceburg's mansion) with a tower; Franky's crew nearby (separate franky_house structure).

## Scale (site 224 x 224)
City radius 96, terraces at y 0 / 12 / 24 / 36, ring canals at r 72-77 and 48-51, fountain to ~y 89.
Dock 1: 68 x 105 with two 21-wide slipways and four 30-high gantries. HQ: 47 x 35, 3 storeys + clock tower.

## Palette roles
base: onepiece:canal_bricks (terrace walls, quoins), water7_cobbles streets; walls: dressrosa_stucco, sandstone,
calcite, bricks; roofs: dressrosa_tile_stairs, brick, mangrove, prismarine; accents: red gantries, gold clock,
light-blue glass jets, lanterns.

## v2 additions
~70 furnished houses round three rings, grand stairs on three axes, bridges on 8 axes, a three-basin fountain with
real water, Dock 1 with workshops, galleon, ribbed hull, gantries, HQ with furnished floors and Iceburg's office,
