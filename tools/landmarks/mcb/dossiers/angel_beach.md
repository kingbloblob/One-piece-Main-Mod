# Angel Beach - dossier (batch 12)
Sources: series knowledge (Skypiea arc ep. 153-160), fandom wiki.
- Angel Island in Skypiea floats on the White-White Sea (Sea Cloud, sailed like water); Angel Beach is its soft cloud beach of Island Cloud with tall thin palms.
- Skypiean houses: round white buildings with domed roofs, round windows, little side domes; pastel blue accents; built on cloud.
- Conis and Pagaya live in a house on the beach slope with a Waver shed; Pagaya builds dials (Tone, Flame, Lamp dials) and Wavers.
- Lovely Street: market street with stalls; Milky Road cloud ribbon curls off into the sky; Going Merry anchored off the beach; Heaven's Gate is far below (not here).
Design brief: 160 site, sky (canvas y 0 = world 168). Main cloud island r~56 with a soft hill; beach of island cloud; a ring of white-white sea (white stained glass, light blue in shallows) out to r~74 held in a thin cloud dish; ~16 round domed cottages along Lovely Street with blue bands and side domes; Conis's house (2 domes, terrace, Waver shed with 3 wavers); dial shop; market stalls; Milky Road ribbon rising in a spiral off the east edge; pier with Going Merry; palms; chests sky_treasure.
