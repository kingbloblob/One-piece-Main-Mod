# Twin Capes (Reverse Mountain exit) - dossier (batch 10)
Sources: WebSearch snippets (Wikipedia "Grand Line", One Piece wiki Crocus page via breezewiki, Netflix Tudum on Laboon); fandom blocked in the cloud box.
- Reverse Mountain sits on the Red Line; the Twin Capes lie at its exit on the Paradise side, the only inhabited spot.
- Crocus (ex Roger Pirates doctor) keeps the lighthouse and looks after Laboon; anime shows him in a deck chair under a parasol reading the paper on the cape.
- Laboon: island whale bigger than the capes, keeps ramming the Red Line, scarred forehead; Luffy painted the Straw Hat jolly roger on it.
- Inside Laboon: Crocus' painted sky (blue walls, white clouds), stomach acid sea, little islet with a house, deck chair, parasol; an iron door in the side.
