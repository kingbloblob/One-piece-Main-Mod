# Egghead (structure id egghead_lab) dossier
Depth: quick (anime/manga memory of the Egghead arc, ch. 1061-1125; web pages blocked).
Scale: site 176 x 176, egg 48 wide and about 64 tall on a 20-high plateau.

## Canon
- Vegapunk's "island of the future": a warm island in winter seas, kept warm by his science.
- Faustphase / Fabriophase below (factories where the Pacifista and Seraphim are made, robot-run town),
  Labophase above: the giant egg-shaped lab with a cracked top, Punk Records (the brain) above it.
- The Straw Hats arrive on the Thousand Sunny; Kizaru leads the Marine attack; Seraphim guard the lab.

## Design choices (flagged)
- A rocky plateau with ledges carries the egg on a flared stalk; a glass escalator climbs the cliff.
- Inside the egg: three lab floors (workshops, the library of knowledge, quarters/control), elevator up the
  middle, a viewing deck at the crack with a glass floor, Punk Records as a two-lobed pink brain on a stem.
- Town: round white pod labs with coloured bands and domes, the Pacifista factory with a sawtooth glass roof and
  assembly line, the round Seraphim hall, a pastel robot cafe, glowing white roads, round pink/green trees.
- Harbour basin with the Thousand Sunny (real ship template).
