# Banaro Island - dossier (batch 12)
Sources: series knowledge (ep. 325, chapter 440-441).
- Small desert-edge island town of plain stone/sandstone houses. Ace (Mera Mera) fought Blackbeard (Yami Yami): fire pillars, Entei; the darkness swallowed whole blocks of buildings; the town was left in ruins, half of it gone.
Design brief: 160 site land: sandy island with dunes; ~30 stone houses, many roofless, burnt (scorched_stone, blackstone, ember_ash, campfire embers), half-swallowed houses sinking into a black hole patch (black concrete/obsidian swirl crater), a scorched fire crater with magma glass edges; ruined clock tower; well; the surviving tavern with the Blackbeard flag; wanted posters; npc blackbeard + blackbeard_pirate guards; harbour pier.
