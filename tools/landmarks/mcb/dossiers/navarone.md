# Navarone (G-8) dossier
Source: anime filler arc G-8 (episodes 196-206), from memory + WebSearch snippets (fandom blocked in the cloud box).
- Marine fortress G-8, "the impregnable fortress": an island of sheer rock cliffs; ships enter through a sea gate
  in the cliff and a tunnel into an inner harbour enclosed by the rock, invisible from the sea.
- The base proper sits on and in the cliffs: white Marine buildings with blue trim/roofs, a big HQ block,
  Commodore Jonathan's office overlooking the inner harbour, communications/radar, gun batteries on the outer cliffs,
  searchlights.
- Jessica runs the famous kitchen and the huge mess hall; Drake (Marine diver), the base doctors (infirmary), the
  dock workers who look after captured ships: the Going Merry was hidden in the harbour and repaired there.
- Signature: ring of cliffs + inner harbour, tunnel + sea gate, white/blue Marine fortress, "MARINE" and G-8 markings.
Build (v2): 176 x 176 site, y 0 = sea. Rim plateau y 30, inner harbour (water y 0, depth 8) reached by a 18-wide
channel through a rock tunnel with a huge two-leaf sea gate; HQ (3 + 2 storeys + Jonathan's office tower + radar)
on the north rim; kitchen + mess hall ground floor; barracks/infirmary/armory/stores; east barracks block, west
depot; 2 stair towers rim->quay; quay with cranes, lamps, bollards; Merry in the repair dock, Marine warship
moored; gun batteries and 4 searchlight towers on the rim.
