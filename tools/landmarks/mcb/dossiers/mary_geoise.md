# Mary Geoise (structure id mary_geoise) dossier
Depth: quick (web pages blocked; search snippets + anime/manga memory of the Reverie arc, ch. 905-908, 1084-1086).
Scale: site 200 x 200, Holy Land plateau 46 above the Red Port.

## Canon
- The Holy Land on top of the Red Line, seat of the World Government and home of the Celestial Dragons; reached from
  the Red Port at the foot of the cliff by the Bondola (a bubble-cabin lift running up the cliff face).
- Pangaea Castle: white palace, the Empty Throne in its great hall with the twenty swords of the founding kings
  driven into the floor round it (ch. 907-908); the Five Elders' room; Im's flower room high up. Reference noted by
  several articles: the French Renaissance Chateau de Chambord (many towers, conical roofs, chimneys, a lantern tower).
- Below the castle a freezer room keeps a giant straw hat (ch. 908). Slaves live and work under the beautiful surface.
- Celestial Dragons' mansions with gardens, fountains and statues; the Holy Gate at the top of the Bondola.

## Design choices (flagged)
White smooth quartz + quartz bricks with gold trim; grey-blue (light-blue terracotta + deepslate tiles) Chambord roofs
with cones; a central lantern tower with a gold dome. Mansions: white, columned porticos, hip roofs, two floors.
Red Port: warehouses, customs house, a walled harbour basin with a real Marine warship template (shipstamp).

## Palette roles
base: red terracotta bands (red/orange/white/terracotta strata) for the Red Line; wall: smooth quartz/quartz bricks;
trim: quartz pillars, gold blocks; roof: light-blue terracotta + cobbled deepslate tiles; accent: gold, flowers.

## Ground (ryan's batch 4 rules)
The mesa slopes down in eroded terraces to y 0 on the east, west and north sides before the site edge (no square
plate, no wall at the site border); the Red Port ground and gardens use noise patches, not patterns.
