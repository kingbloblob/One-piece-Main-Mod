# Yuba - dossier (batch 10)
Sources: WebSearch snippets (breezewiki Yuba, One Piece Odyssey Alabasta story, Episode of Arabasta).
- Small oasis town in the western Sandora Desert; circular, clay-looking buildings. Crocodile's sandstorms nearly buried it and dried the oasis.
- Toto (Vivi's friend Koza's father) stays alone, digging in the dry lake bed for water; Luffy helps, they strike water.
- Former Rebel Army HQ; the rebels had moved to Katorea.
Design brief: 160 site, ground y 1, irregular round outline (no square edge). Dunes blown from the west bury houses (some to the dome), trenches dug to doors; round adobe houses with domes; cracked dry lake bed; Toto's deep pit with ladder and the spring at the bottom; tent, tools, dry well; Toto's inn (flat roof); abandoned rebel camp (torn tents, racks, crates, broken watchtower, rebel flag); crumbling town wall; dead palms.
