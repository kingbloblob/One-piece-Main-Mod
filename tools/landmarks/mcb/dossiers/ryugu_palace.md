# Fish-Man Island / Ryugu Palace (structure id ryugu_palace) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Fish-Man Island arc (ch. 603-653), the old v1 build.

## Recognisable features
- A giant air bubble 10,000 m down on the sea floor, lit by the sunlight the Eve tree carries down; a smaller bubble
  bulging from the top holds Ryugu Palace (in the show it sits on its own bubble above the main one).
- Ryugu Palace: pink/coral and pearl-white, tiered like a wedding cake, round shell towers with spiral tips, a
  big central dome; King Neptune's huge throne hall (the king is giant-sized), the banquet hall.
- The Hard Shell Tower where Princess Shirahoshi hid for ten years: a tall round tower, her huge room at the top.
- Fish-Man District / Gyoverly Hills: round shell houses with bulb roofs, canals, bubble lamps, coral trees, market
  stalls; the Mermaid Cafe (pink shell building with a pool).
- The Sea Forest: a ship graveyard garden with Otohime's grave and the Poneglyph that holds Joy Boy's apology.
- Tubes and bubble "elevators" link the palace to the town; coral reef and kelp outside.

## Scale (site ~220 x 214)
Main bubble rx 104, 70 high, base 64 below the sea floor (top ~6 above it); palace bubble r 40 centred at y -20
(top ~20, kept under the surface of deep oceans). Palace hill plateau r 38, 32 above the town floor; palace 4 tiers of 8 (49, 43, 37, 31 wide), dome r 14,
four corner shell towers r4, Hard Shell Tower r6 (~26 high). Town rings at r 70 / 84 / 96, canal ring r 50-56.

## Palette roles
bubble: onepiece:bubble_glass; base: sandstone + coral_bricks cliff; walls: coral_bricks, pearl_block; trim:
prismarine bricks, dark prismarine, pearl stairs; accent: bubble_lamp, sea lantern, pink/cyan wool, gold throne.
