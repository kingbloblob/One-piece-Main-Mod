# Weatheria - dossier (batch 11)
Sources: series knowledge (Nami's time skip, chapter covers).
- Small sky island of weather scientists led by old Haredas; islets held together by chains; round buildings with domes; weather balls floating; Nami studies weather science in the library and lab.
Design brief: ~140 site, sky (canvas y 0 = world 168); 7 cloud islets (cloud bowls with puffs), plank rope bridges + heavy chains; main islet with the two-floor Weather Library rotunda, Haredas' lab, the observatory tower with gallery, lens room and great weathervane; houses on islets, kitchen garden, launch pad with a big weather ball, bell; ~22 tethered weather balls.
