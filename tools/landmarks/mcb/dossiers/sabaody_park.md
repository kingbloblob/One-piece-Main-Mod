# Sabaody Park (structure id sabaody_park) dossier

Sources: anime/manga memory of the Sabaody arc (ch. 496-513), the old v1 build.

## Recognisable features
- The amusement park of the Sabaody Archipelago among the giant Yarukiman mangroves, soap bubbles rising from roots.
- The giant Ferris wheel towering over the mangroves (white double rim, colourful cabins); a roller coaster on
  timber trestles with a loop; carousel; bubble ride; the park gate with the SABAODY PARK sign; stalls and food.

## Scale (site 156 x 150, v1 104 x 100)
Ferris wheel radius 30 (hub ~37 up); coaster oval 52 x 64 round the carousel; big top r 13; drop tower 44 high.

## Palette roles
paths: smooth quartz/calcite with pink kerbs; rides: white/pink concrete, red track, birch trestles; buildings:
pastel terracotta with birch/cherry frames and cherry/prismarine/red nether roofs; accent: onepiece:bubble_glass,
bubble lamps, sea lanterns, striped wool awnings.

## v2 additions
Circus big top with ring and tiered seats, drop tower, swing ride, open-air show stage with benches, umbrella food
court, haunted house (cobwebs, pumpkins, iron fence), 5 furnished 1-2 floor park buildings (ticket hall, cafe,
staff house, prize shop, game hall), more stalls, lamp-lit avenues, 7 Yarukiman trees, 70 bubble spots.
