# Marineford (Marine Headquarters) dossier

Sources: One Piece wiki snippets via search (fandom pages are blocked in the cloud box), anime memory of the
Summit War arc (ep. 459-489), the old v1 build in tools/landmarks/marineford.py.

## What makes it recognisable
- Crescent island around a round bay that opens to the sea. Brick + steel, cannons along the edges.
- Marine HQ at the back: a huge stepped white fortress, every tier capped with blue Japanese-castle eaves,
  a castle keep (tenshu) on top with a gold ridge ornament. The front of the lower block carries the big
  海 [gull] 軍 sign ("Kaigun", Marines) and the seagull emblem.
- The execution scaffold in front of the HQ: a tall white tower standing on a high wall; the three admirals
  sit on top of that wall, looking down on Oris Plaza.
- Oris Plaza: the wide paved square between the wall and the bay. The Ox Bell stands at its front.
- The Encircling Wall: steel panels that rise from the bay edge (shown raised), cannons in the upper windows.
- A town around the plaza on the crescent arms (Marine families live there), watchtowers at the bay mouth.

## Scale (1 block = 1 m, capped by the 116-block jigsaw reach)
Site 220 x 220. HQ 113 wide, 67 deep, 4 tiers + keep, roof ridge ~108. Scaffold tower ~62 tall on a 17 high
wall. Bay circle r 100 opening south. Encircling Wall 26 tall (from the bay floor).

## Palette roles
base: onepiece:marine_bricks / polished andesite plinth; wall: marine_bricks + white concrete mix; trim:
onepiece:marine_pillar, blue terracotta bands; roof: onepiece:wano_roof_tiles (+ stairs), blue; accent:
gold block ridge fish, lanterns, blue banners, light-blue glass.

## Signature details
1. 海 gull 軍 sign + gull emblem over the main gate.
2. Scaffold tower with the admirals' seats on the wall below it.
3. Raised Encircling Wall with cannons, Ox Bell on the plaza.
