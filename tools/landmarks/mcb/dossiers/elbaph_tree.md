# Elbaph (structure id elbaph_tree) dossier
Depth: quick (manga memory of the Elbaph arc, ch. 1126+; web pages blocked).
Scale: site 176 x 176, trunk about 30 across and 108 tall, crown about 110 across.

## Canon
- Land of the giants in a cold northern sea; the Treasure Tree Adam so vast its crown is a land of its own.
- Giants' Viking-like culture: longhouses, feasting, warriors' training, giant weapons, rune stones; the Owl Library.

## Design choices (flagged)
- The tree: adam_log trunk with flared base, roots arching over the ground, a giants' doorway, a spiral stair along
  the inner wall to the Owl Library (three galleries of books round a wooden owl) and the Lookout Hall, which opens
  onto the first great bough with a railed platform. Eight great branches carry leaf clouds.
- Village built to giant size (longhouses 37-41 long, 14-high walls, giant tables and benches, hearths, barrels),
  plus human-size guest cottages and a loft in each longhouse.
- Training ring with a giant sword, axe and shield; five rune stones; a long jetty (no ship: no real giant ship
  template exists yet).

## Ground (ryan's batch 4 rules)
Snowy pine fells in the north-west, green valley in the south, noise-patch cover, giant ferns/barley/clover in drifts.
